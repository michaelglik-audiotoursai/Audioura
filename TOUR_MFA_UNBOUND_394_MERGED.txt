Positioned within the thematic exploration of books as artworks, this piece invites you to consider the intersection of image, text, typography, paper, and binding as one integrated entity.

The exhibit of "Au Soleil du Plafond" is a poetic and thought-provoking exploration of the artistic collaboration between Juan Gris, the Spanish painter of Cubism, and Pierre Reverdy. Through their joint efforts, this livre d'artiste challenges conventional artistic boundaries by seamlessly blending image and text into a harmonious whole. The collaboration delves into the realm where visual art and literary expression coalesce, inviting viewers to contemplate the interplay between various forms of creative expression. In this unique exhibition, visitors are immersed in the world of livres d'artiste, where the book itself becomes the artwork. Through the lens of Gris and Reverdy's collaboration, the seamless integration of images and words takes center stage, transforming the traditional book format into a multi-dimensional work of art.
