# Cases (D726 amended):
#   REJECT   — incorrect content (not a place / not this venue / wrong facts):
#              move OUT of stop_pool into stop_pool_rejected (reject_stops.py --apply).
#   FIX      — wrong pin (real place, wrong coordinates): correct in place
#              (fix_stop_coords.py --apply). Stop STAYS in the pool.
#   RE-FILE  — filed under the wrong venue but resolves to one real place: re-file
#              under its own venue identity; if it does not resolve, treat as REJECT.
#   NOTHING  — out of scope for THIS tour only (real place, too far): keep it in the
#              pool; it was already skip-only (LOCAL-788). No command.
