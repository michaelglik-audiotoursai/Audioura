Your first stop is Le Lézard aux plumes d’or (The Lizard with Golden Feathers).

Joan Miró's "Le Lézard aux plumes d’or" is an illustrated book with 40 color lithographs bound in publisher vellum, gifted. Each lithograph showcases Miró's playful and surreal interpretation of a mythical lizard adorned with golden feathers against a dreamlike backdrop. Miró's artistic style, characterized by bold lines and whimsical shapes, challenges traditional perceptions of reality. The fusion of image, text, and typography blurs boundaries between visual art and literature, creating an immersive experience. Miró's work transforms the ordinary into the extraordinary, where the fantastical meets the familiar, redefining reality through his artistic lens. This piece showcases Miró's mastery of color and form, illustrating the limitless possibilities of artistic expression.
