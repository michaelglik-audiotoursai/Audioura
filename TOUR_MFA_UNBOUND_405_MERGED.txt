From this vantage point, you'll notice the intricate details and vibrant colors of this unique piece that challenge traditional representations of mythical creatures.

Nestled within this exhibition is "Le Lézard aux plumes d’or," a captivating illustrated book featuring 40 color lithographs by Joan Miró. The book is bound in publisher’s vellum and was published by Louis Broder, a French publisher of art books, with printing by Mourlot Frères. Generously gifted this remarkable creation to the museum. This work embodies the exhibition's thesis that the book itself can be considered a singular artwork, integrating image, text, typography, paper, and binding into a cohesive whole. Each lithograph within the book showcases Miró's masterful use of color, line, and form to depict a mythical lizard adorned with shimmering golden feathers. The unconventional depiction challenges viewers to reconsider their perceptions of nature and mythology, inviting contemplation on the boundaries between reality and fantasy. Miró's innovative approach to combining image and text in this work had no precedent and revolutionized the book as an art form, highlighting the collaborative efforts of the Louis Broder, printer, and donor in bringing this extraordinary piece to life.
