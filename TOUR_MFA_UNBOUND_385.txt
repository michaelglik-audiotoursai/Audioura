Your first stop is Le Lézard aux plumes d’or (The Lizard with Golden Feathers).

In front of you lies a mesmerizing creation by Joan Miró, dating back to 1971. "Le Lézard aux plumes d’or" is an illustrated book featuring 40 color lithographs, each bursting with a kaleidoscope of hues. The lithographs are enclosed in a cover made of publisher’s vellum, adding a touch of elegance to the vibrant imagery within. Miró's playful yet profound exploration of surrealism is evident in the whimsical creatures and abstract forms dancing across the pages. This work challenges conventional perceptions, blurring the lines between reality and fantasy, inviting viewers to delve into a world where imagination knows no bounds. The visual feast presented in this illustrated book transcends traditional storytelling, offering a visual narrative that sparks curiosity and wonder. Miró's masterful use of color and form invites you to explore the depths of your own imagination, urging you to seek meaning beyond the boundaries of the visible. Gifted this piece stands as a testament to Miró's artistic genius and his ability to transport viewers to a realm where the ordinary transforms into the extraordinary.

Stop 3: Au Soleil du Plafond

Address: 465 Huntington Ave, Boston, MA 02115

"Au Soleil du Plafond" is a mesmerizing piece that unfolds a world of visual poetry. Within its pages, Gris and Reverdy masterfully blend art and literature, creating a harmonious fusion of colors and words. Each stroke of the brush and each carefully chosen word come together to form a narrative that transcends the ordinary. The work immerses visitors in a realm where art and literature intertwine, inviting exploration of boundless possibilities of expression. This piece not only showcases the artistic talent of Juan Gris, a cubist painter and sculptor, but also highlights the magic that unfolds when different forms of art converge. The historical context of the collaboration between Gris and Reverdy adds another layer of depth to the work, inviting you to ponder the interconnectedness of creativity across disciplines. "Au Soleil du Plafond" stands as a testament to the enduring power of art to inspire, delight, and provoke thought. This unique fusion of art and literature leaves a lasting impression on our perception of the world.
