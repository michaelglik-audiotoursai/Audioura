Stop 1: Ancient Nubia Now

Museum Information: 10:00–05:00. FREE

Orientation: In the heart of the Museum of Fine Arts, Boston, "Ancient Nubia Now" unfolds a mesmerizing tapestry of history and culture. Stand before the intricately carved royal statues and gleaming jewelry to appreciate the artistry of a civilization that once rivaled Egypt.

The "Ancient Nubia Now" exhibition at the Museum of Fine Arts, Boston, showcases the grandeur and complexity of ancient Nubian civilization. This display includes stunning Kerma pottery, colossal statues kings, and exquisite jewels that highlight the craftsmanship of Nubian artisans. Nubia's rich trade networks stretched across the Mediterranean, influencing cultures from Greece to Rome. This exhibition, supported by contributions from donors like Photographs and Landon, aims to rewrite historical misinterpretations about Nubia.
