# Audio Description: *Springtime* (1872)

**Orientation**

You're standing before *Springtime*, painted in 1872 by Claude Monet. Start a few steps back, where the whole canvas fits comfortably in your view. From there, the painting reads as a single scene. Then move closer to see how that scene is built up on the surface.

**The Work and the Artist**

*Springtime* is an 1872 painting by Claude Monet. Its title, a single word naming the season, tells you what the artist chose as his subject.

The title is shared by other works. The French painter Pierre Auguste Cot completed his own *Springtime* in 1873, just a year after this one. The American painter Wilbur Winfield Woodward also painted a *Springtime* later in the nineteenth century. So the work in front of you is the earliest of three nineteenth-century paintings that share this name. That small fact shows how often painters of the period returned to spring as a subject.

**The Emotional Reading**

The work rests on a plain idea: one season, set down by Monet in a single year, 1872.
