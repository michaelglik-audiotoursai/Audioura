# Portrait of Comtesse d'Haussonville: Audio Description

**Orientation**

Stand close to *Portrait of Comtesse d'Haussonville*, about an arm's length away, and look past the young woman's shoulder into the mirror behind her. From here you can see what a distant glance misses: the back of her head reflected in the glass, a crimson ribbon tied in her hair that is invisible from the front, and the cluttered dresser beneath it. Then step back several paces. From a distance the strangeness of her right arm emerges, too long and too supple to belong to a real body.

**The Work**

This is an oil-on-canvas portrait, signed and dated 1845 at the lower left. The sitter is Louise de Broglie, Countess d'Haussonville, aged twenty-seven. She came from the wealthy House of Broglie and would later become an essayist, a biographer and a writer of historical romance novels.
