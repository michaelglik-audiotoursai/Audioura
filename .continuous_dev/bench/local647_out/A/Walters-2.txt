*The Artist*:  
Lawrence Alma-Tadema was a Dutch-born, British-based painter known for his meticulous reconstructions of classical antiquity. By 1881, Alma-Tadema had established himself as a leading interpreter of ancient life, blending archaeological precision with imaginative storytelling. With *Sappho and Alcaeus*, he was at a turning point: newly settled in London, he was pushing his technique further, using oil paint to evoke the luminous surfaces of stone, sea, and skin, and experimenting with the interplay of public spectacle and private emotion. The work reflects his fascination with the everyday lives and rituals of the ancient world, and his desire to bridge scholarly research with popular appeal.
