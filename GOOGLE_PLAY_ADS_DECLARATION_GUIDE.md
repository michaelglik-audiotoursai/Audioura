## Question: Does Your App Contain Ads?

**Location in Google Play Console:** App Content → Ads

---

## What Google Means by "Ads"

### Ads (requires "Yes" selection):
- Third-party ad networks delivering ads to users
- Google AdMob (banner, interstitial, rewarded video)
- Facebook Audience Network
- AppLovin
- Other external ad vendors
- In-app purchases managed by third parties

### NOT Ads (can select "No"):
- In-app coupons and promotional offers you control
- Direct partnerships with other businesses
- Your own product/service promotions
- Links to partner websites that you manage
- Newsletter/email promotions
- Community events or contests

---

## Audioura's Answer: "No, my app does not contain ads"

### Current Status (June 2026):
- ✅ No third-party ad networks
- ✅ No Google AdMob
- ✅ No external ad vendors
- ✅ No monetization via ads

### Future Plans (After Launch):
- "Treats" tab will include coupons and offers controlled by Audioura
- These are **NOT** considered "ads" in Google's definition
- Can be added without changing this declaration

---

## Implications of "No"

### If You Select "No":
- ✅ App does not get "Contains ads" label
- ✅ Better store placement (users who avoid ad-heavy apps can find you)
- ✅ No impact on coupons/offers in "Treats" tab
- ⚠️ If you later add third-party ad networks (e.g., Google AdMob), you MUST update this declaration and resubmit for review

### If You Select "Yes":
- ❌ App labeled "Contains ads" on store listing
- ❌ Slightly lower placement (apps with ads are less discoverable)
- ❌ Unnecessary until you use ad networks

---

## When to Update This Declaration

**Update from "No" → "Yes" only if you:**
- Add Google AdMob
- Add Facebook Audience Network
- Add AppLovin or other ad network
- Use any third-party service that delivers ads to users

**When to update:**
- Before resubmitting your app for review
- Not required for coupons, partnerships, or in-app promotions
