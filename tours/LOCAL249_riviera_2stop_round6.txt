Step-by-Step Audio Guided Tour: French Riviera cycling tour, France - Cycling Tour
Tour-Category: walking

Stop 1: Cap d'Antibes

Address: Cap d'Antibes, 06160 Antibes, France

Coordinates: 43.5486, 7.1237

Type/Specialty: Coastal Scenery

Specific Examples: Beautiful beaches, scenic coastal views, luxury villas

Orientation: Start biking southeast on the main road, enjoy the sea breeze along the way. As you arrive at Cap d'Antibes on your French Riviera cycling tour, take a moment to appreciate the stunning views of the Mediterranean Sea surrounding this picturesque peninsula.

Beneath the lavish mansions perched along the cap lies a hidden network of smuggler's tunnels that once played a role in wartime espionage. In 2023, Antibes boasted a population of 77,637, making it the second most populous commune in Alpes-Maritimes after Nice. Standing at Cap d'Antibes, the gentle sea breeze carries the scent of saltwater and pine trees, immersing visitors in the sensory richness of the French Riviera. The sound of waves lapping against the rocky shores creates a soothing backdrop to the historical narratives embedded in the rugged terrain. Cycle towards the tip of the cap, where the coastline stretches out before you, inviting curiosity and wonder.

Directions: As you leave Cap d'Antibes, head east along the picturesque coastal road enjoying views of the Mediterranean Sea. Follow the scenic route towards Nice, passing through the charming town of Villefranche-sur-Mer before reaching the stunning peninsula of Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat. Keep an eye out for the iconic Villa Ephrussi de Rothschild along the way—it's a must-see!

Stop 2: Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat

Address: 06230 Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat, France

Coordinates: 43.6804, 7.3194

Type/Specialty: Luxurious Gardens

Specific Examples: Exotic plants, stunning landscapes, panoramic sea views

Orientation: As you arrive at Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat on your French Riviera cycling tour, take in the stunning views of the Mediterranean Sea stretching out before you. Look to your left to see the charming town nestled on the peninsula, surrounded by lush greenery and grand villas. The gentle sea breeze carries with it the scent of saltwater and pine, setting the tranquil atmosphere for your exploration.

Pedaling through Saint-Jean-Cap-Ferrat reveals a town with a rich history dating back to ancient Greek times. The cobblestone streets and elegant architecture echo this past. The opulent villas along the coast have attracted European aristocracy and the global elite seeking respite in the warm climate. This area, known as the "Billionaires' Peninsula," exudes tranquility and luxury that has drawn visitors for centuries. Its eclectic gardens, a fusion of styles and influences, speak to the cross-pollination of ideas that have flourished in this coastal paradise.
