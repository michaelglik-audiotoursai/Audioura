Stop 1: Cap d'Antibes

Address: Cap d'Antibes, 06160 Antibes, France

Coordinates: 43.5411, 7.1213

Type/Specialty: Coastal Scenery

Specific Examples: Beautiful beaches, luxury villas, scenic viewpoints

Orientation: Start at Antibes train station, head south on Avenue de Cannes, follow signs for Cap d'Antibes. Enjoy the coastal breeze as you cycle along the waterfront. As you arrive at Cap d'Antibes, the gentle sound of waves crashing against the rocky shore fills the air. To your right, the imposing walls of Château de la Croë stand as a testament to the opulence that once graced this captivating cape.

You are about to embark on a journey through the French Riviera, a tale of contrasts woven between the sparkling coastline and the ancient hilltop villages. As you cycle along the Cap d'Antibes, the opulent châteaux whisper stories of writers and artists who once sought solace amidst their grandeur. In Eze Village, narrow cobblestone streets lead to the Jardin Exotique, offering a panoramic view that hints at the village's medieval past as a fortified stronghold. The secrets of this region lie hidden between the glitz of today and the forgotten histories of yesteryears, waiting for you to unravel their intricacies. This tour is a book of chapters waiting to be explored, each stop revealing a different facet of the French Riviera's rich tapestry.

Description:
Cap d'Antibes, a stunning peninsula located to the south of Antibes and east of Juan-les-Pins on the French Riviera, holds a rich historical and cultural significance. In January 1888, renowned artist Claude Monet journeyed to this picturesque location seeking inspiration. Monet, following the advice of his friend Guy de Maupassant, resided at Château de la Pinède, a haven for artists, where he marveled at the beauty of the Mediterranean coast. The Tire-Poil coastal path offers visitors a sensory feast as they traverse the cape, starting from the Garoupe beach parking lot to the iconic Villa Eilenroc. This 2.7 km trail winds along the rugged coastline, passing by the grand walls of Château de la Garoupe and Château de la Croë. The path affords breathtaking views of the Lérins Islands to the west and the Mercantour mountains to the east, providing a glimpse into the natural splendor that has inspired artists for generations. At the highest point of the cape stands the Garoupe lighthouse, overlooking the Golfe Juan and the Bay of Angels, offering a panoramic vista of the azure waters below. Nearby, the ancient chapel of Notre Dame de Bon Port and the harbor of Olivette add layers of history to this enchanting landscape. As you cycle along the Cap d'Antibes, the echoes of literary giants like F. Scott Fitzgerald, who drew inspiration from this very coastline for his novel "Tender Is the Night," whisper through the sea breeze. The legacy of writers and artists who sought solace and creativity in these sun-kissed shores adds a timeless allure to this idyllic location. As you depart the shimmering coastline, the hills ahead beckon with promises of hidden stories waiting to be unveiled, inviting you to continue your journey through the rich tapestry of history that defines the French Riviera.

Directions: Head east from Cap d'Antibes towards Antibes town center and follow signs to Juan-les-Pins. Once in Juan-les-Pins, continue heading east along the coast towards Nice. You'll pass through Villefranche-sur-Mer before reaching Eze Village, perched on the cliffs overlooking the Mediterranean Sea.

Stop 2: Eze Village

Address: 06360 Èze, France

Coordinates: 43.7272, 7.3619

Type/Specialty: Medieval Charm

Specific Examples: Narrow cobblestone streets, artisan shops, exotic gardens

Orientation: As you pedal through the picturesque French Riviera, your journey leads you to the ancient marvel of Eze Village. Perched atop a rocky hill overlooking the azure waters of the Mediterranean, this historic village offers a glimpse into the past with its preserved medieval charm. To truly appreciate the essence of Eze Village, make your way to the Jardin Exotique for a breathtaking view that encapsulates the village's storied history.

In the heart of Eze Village, cobblestone streets wind through ancient stone buildings, evoking a sense of timelessness. This village's historical significance dates back to around 200 BC when it was established near Mount Bastide. The maritime section of the Antonine Itinerary provides the earliest recorded mention of the area, referring to the bay of Eze as Avisionis portus, hinting at its strategic coastal location. Amidst the narrow alleys and stone structures, a symphony of scents fills the air, blending the fragrance of Mediterranean flora with the salty tang of the sea breeze. The sound of church bells echoes through the village, adding a melodic layer to the ancient ambiance of Eze. Eze Village's connection to our tour theme lies in its artists' legacy, as many renowned painters and writers have found inspiration in its timeless beauty. The Jardin Exotique, a botanical garden perched on a cliff, offers a sensory feast with exotic plants and panoramic views that have captivated creatives through the centuries. As you stand at the edge of the Jardin Exotique, gazing over the endless expanse of the Mediterranean, the ancient stones beneath your feet whisper tales of conquest and resilience. In Eze Village, the weight of centuries hangs palpably in the air, inviting you to uncover the mysteries hidden within its time-worn walls.
