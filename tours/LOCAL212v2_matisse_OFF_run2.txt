Stop 1: Nu bleu IV

Address: 164 Avenue des Arènes de Cimiez, 06000 Nice, France

Coordinates: 43.7196, 7.2755

Museum Information: Closed on Tuesday. 10:00–17:00 (1 Nov–31 Mar); 10:00–18:00 (1 Apr–31 Oct). €12; free for Métropole residents

You are about to embark on a journey through the legacy of Henri Matisse's donations at the Musée Matisse, a narrative woven from the threads of his personal contributions. As you step into the seventeenth-century Villa des Arènes, inaugurated as the museum in 1963, you'll delve into the vibrant world shaped by Matisse's pioneering artistry. Each chapter of this exploration unveils a different facet of his influence, from the groundbreaking 'Nu bleu IV' to the enchanting 'Nymphe dans la forêt', symbolizing the evolution of the museum's collection. Discover how Matisse's generosity continues to resonate, culminating in the 2025 donation that expanded the museum's narrative, inviting you to unravel the mysteries of his enduring impact.

Nu bleu IV, created by Henri Matisse in 1952, is a striking female nude rendered in blue gouache on paper, cut and pasted onto a white Canson paper mounted on canvas. The bold use of color and innovative technique showcase Matisse's pioneering approach to form and composition. This masterpiece is one of four Blue Nudes by the artist, embodying a unique blend of simplicity and sophistication. Embedded within this artwork is Matisse's exploration of the human figure and his revolutionary use of color to convey emotion and vitality. Each curve and contour of the nude figure is meticulously crafted to evoke a sense of movement and grace, inviting viewers to contemplate the beauty of the female form in a new light. Nu bleu IV, received as a donation in 1979, holds a special place in the Musée Matisse's collection, symbolizing the artist's enduring legacy and influence on modern art. As a cornerstone of the museum's holdings, this piece exemplifies Matisse's commitment to pushing artistic boundaries and redefining traditional conventions. This extraordinary artwork not only captivates the eye with its bold aesthetics but also offers a glimpse into Matisse's innovative vision and artistic evolution. Nu bleu IV stands as a testament to the artist's mastery of color and form, showcasing his ability to capture the essence of the human form with unparalleled creativity and expression.

Stop 2: Nymphe dans la forêt

Address: 164 Avenue des Arènes de Cimiez, 06000 Nice, France

With a brush that traversed the realms of color and draughtsmanship, Henri Matisse created 'Nymphe dans la forêt' between 1936 and 1938. This departure from his typical style delves into mythological subject matter, depicting a naked nymph in a forest, approached by a satyr. The scene is imbued with a sense of mystery and allure, inviting viewers to immerse themselves in a world of myth and legend. Matisse's masterful technique shines through in this piece, with each brushstroke carefully placed to convey emotion and movement. The lush greenery of the forest envelops the central figures, creating a sense of intimacy and secrecy in this enchanted setting. At a deeper level, 'Nymphe dans la forêt' can be seen as a reflection of Matisse's fascination with the intersection of reality and fantasy, where the natural world merges with the realm of the supernatural. The painting invites contemplation on the power of myth and storytelling in shaping our understanding of the world around us. As part of Musee Matisse's collection, 'Nymphe dans la forêt' adds a layer of complexity to the museum's narrative, showcasing Matisse's versatility and willingness to explore new artistic territories. This significant piece not only stands as a testament to the artist's creative evolution but also enriches the museum's offerings, providing visitors with a window into Matisse's exploration of mythology and imagination. Standing before 'Nymphe dans la forêt', one cannot help but be drawn into its mystical world, where the boundaries between reality and fantasy blur, and the whispers of ancient tales echo through the centuries. This painting, with its timeless allure, is a testament to Matisse's enduring legacy and the ongoing journey of discovery that awaits all who engage with his work.
