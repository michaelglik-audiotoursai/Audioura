Stop 1: Cap d'Antibes

Address: Cap d'Antibes, 06160 Antibes, France

Coordinates: 43.5411, 7.1250

Type/Specialty: Coastal Scenery

Specific Examples: Beautiful beaches, scenic cliffs, luxurious villas

Orientation: Start cycling southeast on the main coastal road, enjoy the sea breeze along the way. As you arrive at Cap d'Antibes on your French Riviera cycling tour, the salty breeze carries the sweet aroma of blooming flowers mingling with the faint scent of the sea. The ancient olive trees line the coastal road, their silvery leaves whispering stories of opulent gatherings and clandestine rendezvous.

You are about to embark on a journey through the storied landscapes of the French Riviera, a tapestry of opulence and mystery that weaves together the azure allure of the sea with whispered tales of bygone eras. As you cycle through Cap d'Antibes, the ancient olive trees bear witness to centuries of opulent gatherings and secretive trysts, hinting at the hidden histories beneath the sun-drenched glamour. And in the exotic gardens atop Eze Village, the panoramic view that once inspired Nietzsche awaits, a philosopher who walked these very paths contemplating life and existence. Each stop along this journey reveals a different facet of this luxurious realm, where echoes of clandestine gatherings and hidden secrets still linger, waiting to be uncovered by those who dare to explore its winding paths.

The Cap d'Antibes holds a unique charm, with its geographical location at the southern tip of a peninsula between Antibes and Juan-les-Pins. This promontory, along with Cap Ferrat to the northeast, forms a stunning natural landscape that has captured the imagination of artists and travelers alike. In January 1888, the renowned painter Claude Monet visited the Cap d'Antibes during his sojourn in the south of France. At the recommendation of his friend Guy de Maupassant, Monet resided at the Château de la Pinède, a haven for artists, where he found inspiration in the azure waters and rugged coastline. Wandering along the "Tire-Poil" coastal trail, your footsteps echo against the rocky outcrops, leading you past the historic landmarks of Château de la Garoupe and Château de la Croë. The path offers breathtaking views of the Lérins Islands to the west and the Mercantour mountains to the east, while the Garoupe lighthouse stands sentinel over the azure waters of the Gulf of Juan and the Bay of Angels. The Abri de l'Olivette is a picturesque harbor on the western coast of the cape where traditional local boats anchor. The sounds of seagulls overhead and the gentle lapping of waves against the cliffs create a symphony of nature that has resonated through the ages. Cycling through Cap d'Antibes, you are immersed in a rich tapestry of history and natural beauty, where each pedal stroke uncovers secrets of the past and promises of new adventures on the horizon.

Directions: Head northeast from Cap d'Antibes towards Antibes city center. Follow the coastline with stunning views of the Mediterranean Sea until you reach the train station. From there, take a train towards Eze Village and enjoy the scenic ride through the French Riviera.

Stop 2: Eze Village

Address: 06360 Èze, France

Coordinates: 43.7272, 7.3617

Type/Specialty: Medieval Village

Specific Examples: Narrow cobblestone streets, artisan shops, panoramic views

Orientation: As you arrive at Eze Village, perched high above the French Riviera, find yourself amidst ancient cobblestone streets and charming stone buildings. Look out for the exotic gardens that crown the village, offering a breathtaking panoramic view of the azure Mediterranean Sea.

Eze Village, dating back to around 200 BC, holds the echoes of centuries past within its fortified walls. The earliest mention of this area can be traced in the maritime section of the Antonine Itinerary, which speaks of the bay of Eze as Avisionis portus. Ancient inhabitants navigated these steep paths, their lives intertwined with the rugged terrain. One concrete sensory detail in Eze Village is the scent of blooming flowers mingling with the salty sea breeze, creating a heady fragrance that lingers in the air. The sound of church bells tolling in the distance adds a timeless quality, connecting to the village's enduring history. Eze Village connects to the theme of our French Riviera cycling tour by embodying the essence of a bygone era, where the past whispers through the stones and the views stretch out towards the horizon. This stop offers a glimpse into the rich tapestry of history and culture that defines this region, inviting you to ponder the passage of time and the resilience of human civilization.
