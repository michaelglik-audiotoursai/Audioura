Step-by-Step Audio Guided Tour: Musee Matisse, Nice, France - Museum Tour
Tour-Category: museum

Stop 1: Blue Nude IV

Address: 164 Avenue des Arènes de Cimiez, 06000 Nice, France

Coordinates: 43.7196, 7.2755

Type/Specialty: Painting

Specific Examples: Visitors will see a masterpiece by Henri Matisse depicting a blue nude figure.

Museum Information: Open from 10am to 6pm. Closed on Tuesdays. Admission fee required.

Orientation: You are about to embark on a captivating journey through the Musée Matisse, a collection intricately woven with the tapestry of Henri Matisse's life in Nice. Each chapter of this tour reveals a different facet of the artist's connection to this vibrant city and how it shaped his revolutionary art. From the bold hues of 'Blue Nude IV' mirroring the lively essence of Nice to the interplay of light and shadow influenced by its sunny climate, you will witness the evolution of Matisse's artistic vision. Delve into his transformative voyage to Tahiti and the intimate domestic scenes reflecting personal narratives, all leading to the culmination in 'Nu bleu IV' — a masterpiece uniting global influences with the essence of Matisse's beloved home.

In front of you stands the mesmerizing Blue Nude IV, a captivating example of Henri Matisse's revolutionary Blue Nudes series. Created through a groundbreaking technique of paper cut-outs, this artwork showcases Matisse's adaptability and creativity following his physical limitations post-surgery. The Blue Nude IV presents a nude figure in a reclining pose, rendered in vibrant shades of cerulean blue, contrasting against a background of crisp white. The figure's form is simplified yet expressive, with flowing curves and sharp edges that seem to dance across the paper.

Matisse's innovative spirit is evident in the way he manipulates color and shape to convey a sense of movement and vitality. The deliberate placement of each cut-out fragment creates a harmonious whole, inviting viewers to explore the intricate details of the composition. The Blue Nude IV not only captures the essence of the human form but also reflects Matisse's deep connection to the lively atmosphere of Nice, where he found inspiration in the Mediterranean light and landscape.

Displayed within the museum's modern art section, this piece resonates with the broader collection by highlighting Matisse's exploration of form, color, and space. As you gaze upon this artwork, consider what inspired Matisse to push the boundaries of traditional representation and how his innovative approach continues to inspire artists and art enthusiasts alike.


Directions: Continue exploring Musee Matisse — proceed to Figure à l'ombrelle.



Stop 2: Figure à l'ombrelle

Address: 164 Avenue des Arènes de Cimiez, 06000 Nice, France

Type/Specialty: Painting

Specific Examples: This exhibit showcases Matisse's depiction of a figure with an umbrella.

Orientation: Orientation: As you enter the Musee Matisse in Nice, France, make your way to the central gallery on the second floor. Here, amidst the soft glow of natural light filtering through large windows, you will find yourself face-to-face with a masterpiece that encapsulates Henri Matisse's fascination with light and shadow: Figure à l'ombrelle.

In 1905, Matisse created this striking piece in Collioure, Pyrénées-Orientales. The painting, executed in an oil-on-canvas pointillist style, showcases his versatility in artistic techniques. The scene depicts a figure with an umbrella, set against a backdrop that plays with the interplay of light and shadow. The deliberate use of pointillism adds a sense of dynamism and depth to the composition, inviting viewers to ponder the nuances of color and form.

Matisse's creative process for Figure à l'ombrelle was deeply influenced by the sunny climate of Nice, where he resided at the time. The warm hues and dappled light in the painting evoke a sense of tranquility and contemplation. The artist's mastery in capturing the essence of a moment in time is evident in the careful balance between light and shadow, creating a mesmerizing interplay of contrasts.

This piece is a testament to Matisse's innovative approach to color and form, as well as his ability to evoke emotion through visual storytelling. As you immerse yourself in the intricate details of Figure à l'ombrelle, take note of how Matisse's exploration of light and shadow adds a layer of complexity to the artwork, drawing you into a world where time seems to stand still.

Before you move on, consider how Matisse's artistic journey continued to evolve after his time in Nice. How did his experiences in Tahiti further shape his artistic expression? This question lingers, inviting you to delve deeper into the artist's ever-evolving narrative.


Directions: Your next stop at Musee Matisse: Fenêtre à Tahiti. Ask museum staff for directions.



Stop 3: Fenêtre à Tahiti

Address: 164 Avenue des Arènes de Cimiez, 06000 Nice, France

Type/Specialty: Painting

Specific Examples: Visitors will enjoy Matisse's depiction of a window scene in Tahiti.

Orientation: In front of you stands Henri Matisse's Fenêtre à Tahiti, a window into the artist's pivotal exploration of exotic landscapes in Tahiti. Matisse's unique use of color and original draughtsmanship shines through in this piece, showcasing his masterful skill as both a painter and a printmaker. The artwork, created in 1935, is part of a series of works that reflect Matisse's evolving artistic vision during this period.

The scene depicted in Fenêtre à Tahiti is a lush tropical landscape, with palm trees swaying gently in the breeze and vibrant flowers blooming under the Tahitian sun. Matisse's bold use of color and simplified forms evoke a sense of joy and freedom, transporting viewers to a world of exotic beauty and tranquility.

What makes this piece truly special is its connection to Matisse's experiences abroad, particularly his time in Tahiti. The painting serves as a visual diary of his encounters with new cultures and landscapes, influencing his later works in Nice. Keep an eye out for subtle references to other works in the museum, such as Figure à l'ombrelle, which further enrich your understanding of Matisse's artistic journey.

So, as you gaze upon Fenêtre à Tahiti, let yourself be transported to a world of vibrant colors and exotic beauty, and ponder how Matisse's travels shaped his artistic legacy in the years to come.

Stop 5: Nu bleu IV

Address: 164 Avenue des Arènes de Cimiez, 06000 Nice, France

Type/Specialty: Painting

Specific Examples: Visitors will see Matisse's 'Nu bleu IV', a striking blue nude painting.

Orientation: In 1952, the French painter Henri Matisse completed 'Nu bleu IV', a striking female nude created from blue gouache-covered paper meticulously cut and pasted on a white Canson paper, mounted on a vertical canvas. This piece is the first of the four blue nudes that Matisse began and the last one he finished, showcasing a unique progression in his artistic process.

Matisse's lifelong exploration of form and color culminates in 'Nu bleu IV', revealing his global influences intertwined with his beloved home in Nice. The artwork is a testament to his innovative technique of 'gouaches découpées', where he transformed simple materials into complex, visually stunning compositions. The blue hues evoke a sense of calm and serenity, while the bold lines and shapes exude a powerful yet delicate energy.

'Nu bleu IV' is a pivotal piece in the museum's collection, exemplifying Matisse's revolutionary approach to art and his dedication to pushing the boundaries of traditional representation. This work, along with others like 'Danseuse créole' and 'Fleurs et fruits', showcases the artist's mastery of this final technique. As you gaze upon 'Nu bleu IV', let the weight of centuries of artistic evolution wash over you, connecting you to Matisse's enduring legacy and the timeless beauty of his creations.

For the current location of 'Nu bleu IV' within the museum, kindly inquire with the knowledgeable museum staff, who will guide you to this exquisite masterpiece.
