Stop 1: Chart House

Coordinates: 42.3513, -71.0496

Orientation: Position yourself to best view this location.

The Chart House occupies a historically significant site. John Hancock, a prominent revolutionary leader, observed British warships arriving at Long Wharf on October 1, 1768. Anthony Burns, an escaped enslaved man, was returned to Virginia from this wharf in 1854 amidst protests. The restaurant serves seafood and steak, with meals priced under $30. Reservations are available online or by phone.
