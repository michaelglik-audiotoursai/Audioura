Your first stop is Old Town of Menton. Head east on Promenade du Soleil, then turn left onto Rue Partouneaux. You'll notice the vibrant colors of the buildings lining the streets. Stop at the foot of the cobbled stairway leading up to the Basilica of Saint-Michel-Archange, a striking example of Baroque architecture that dominates the skyline. Look up to appreciate its intricate façade and imagine the historical layers that have shaped this town.

The Old Town of Menton, known in the local dialect as Mentan, is a place where history and culture intertwine. Since the end of the 14th century, this town has stood on the border between the County of Nice, under the Duke of Savoy, and the Republic of Genoa, creating a unique blend of influences. Walking through its narrow, winding streets, echoes of footsteps resonate against centuries-old walls. The town was once an exclave of the Principality of Monaco until the disputed French plebiscite of 1860 integrated it into France. The name "Menton" might trace back to Mons Ottonis, linked to Otton II, the count of Ventimiglia from 1162-1200. This stop on your cycling tour of the French Riviera is a living testament to the region's layered history and the vibrant pulse of its community.

Directions: Start your ride from the Old Town of Menton and pedal eastward along the stunning coastal road. Enjoy the scenic views as you cycle past the picturesque towns of Roquebrune-Cap-Martin and Beausoleil before reaching Eze Village. Look out for the medieval architecture and charming cobblestone streets as you arrive at your destination.

Orientation: As you cycle along the winding roads of the French Riviera, Eze Village emerges from the landscape like a sentinel of history.

Eze Village, a commune dating back to 200 BC, stands as a timeless guardian overlooking the Mediterranean. Its name first appeared in the maritime section of the Antonine Itinerary as Avisionis portus, a crucial navigational point in antiquity. In 2018, the village was home to 2,225 inhabitants, known as Ezasques, who continue to uphold its rich heritage. The texture of weathered stone underfoot echoes centuries of footsteps, each marking the passage of time from Roman settlers to modern-day wanderers. The exotic garden at the summit of Eze offers a view that once served as a lookout for sentinels, ever vigilant against invaders. The connection to the Old Town of Menton becomes palpable here, as both share a history of strategic importance and cultural richness along this storied coastline. Eze Village embodies the theme of this French Riviera cycling tour: the seamless blend of natural beauty and historical depth. Here, the ancient and the present coexist, inviting reflection on the narratives etched into its stones.
