Stop 1: Cap d'Antibes

Address: Cap d'Antibes, 06160 Antibes, France

Coordinates: 43.5410, 7.1253

Orientation: Stand at the edge of the rocky promontory at Cap d'Antibes, feeling the cool breeze off the Mediterranean Sea as it dances through the fragrant pine trees. Look out towards the azure waters, where the untouched natural beauty of the cape meets the opulent villas that line the coast.

You are about to embark on a journey through the hidden chapters of the French Riviera, where the secrets of opulence and intrigue lie beneath the sunlit shores of Cap d'Antibes. As you stand on the rocky promontory, the untouched natural beauty contrasts with the opulent villas that line the coast, each whispering tales of past extravagance. From the grandeur of 19th-century mansions to the stories of artists and aristocrats who once sought refuge in this picturesque haven, each stop on this tour unveils a different facet of this glamorous tapestry. Brace yourself for a narrative woven from the whispers of the past, where every corner holds a piece of history waiting to be discovered.

At the Cap d'Antibes, the rich history of this stunning location unfolds. In January 1888, Claude Monet, the renowned artist, sought inspiration along the sun-kissed shores of the French Riviera. Staying at the Château de la Pinède, he absorbed the vibrant colors and unique light that would influence his iconic Impressionist works. The "Tire-Poil" coastal path winds along the cape, offering breathtaking views of the sea and surrounding landscapes. The trail passes the imposing walls of the Château de la Garoupe and the Château de la Croë, each guarding centuries of stories within their stone walls. The gentle waves lap against the rocks as you gaze west towards the serene Îles de Lérins and east to the majestic peaks of the Mercantour. At the highest point of the cape stands the Garoupe lighthouse, a beacon overlooking the Gulf of Juan and the Bay of Angels. Nearby, the ancient chapel of Notre Dame de Bon Port stands as a testament to the enduring spiritual significance of this place. As you continue along the path, you'll come across the Abri de l'Olivette, a sheltered harbor where traditional local boats rest peacefully against the rugged coastline. The Cap d'Antibes encapsulates a harmonious blend of natural wonder and human ingenuity, where history whispers through the rocky cliffs and elegant estates. Feel the curiosity stir within you as you imagine the hidden stories waiting to be uncovered in the secluded coves that dot this picturesque landscape.
