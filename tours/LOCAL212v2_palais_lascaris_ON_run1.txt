Stop 1: Raquel

Address: Palais Lascaris, 15 Rue Droite, 06300 Nice, France

Coordinates: 43.6961, 7.2766

Museum Information: 14:00–21:00

Step through time as you embark on a journey through the Palais Lascaris, a sanctuary for musical treasures and tales of love. Built in the seventeenth century and once owned by the Vintimille-Lascaris family, this palace holds secrets of a bygone era. Rumors swirl of Raquel, a legendary figure orchestrating clandestine concerts within these walls. Discover a rare 1717 instrument, a masterpiece believed to have serenaded the halls during whispered gatherings. Restored in the 1960s, the Palais now unveils its rich history to all who enter. Let the transformative power of music and love guide you through each chapter of this immersive experience, where each stop reveals a different facet of its enigmatic past.

The exhibit of Raquel at Palais Lascaris presents a collection of rare and enigmatic works. One particular piece within this exhibit stands out—a stunning portrait depicting a mysterious woman surrounded by lush greenery and vibrant flowers. The artist's attention to detail is immediately striking, with each brushstroke carefully capturing the essence of the subject. In this portrait, the use of chiaroscuro creates a sense of depth and drama, enhancing the enigmatic quality of the woman's expression. Her gaze seems to follow you as you move around the room, inviting you to unravel the secrets hidden within the painting. The choice of colors, from the deep blues of her gown to the rich greens of the foliage, adds to the overall sense of mystery and allure. Historically, this portrait is believed to have been created during a time of great artistic experimentation and cultural exchange. The artist's mastery of light and shadow reflects the influence of both Italian and Flemish painting techniques, blending tradition with innovation in a unique and compelling way. This piece serves as a window into the artistic landscape of its time, offering a glimpse into the complexities of artistic expression during this period. As you explore the Raquel exhibit further, you will discover how this portrait fits within the broader collection at Palais Lascaris. Each work in the exhibit tells a story, but this particular piece leaves a lasting impression, drawing you in with its beauty and mystery. What musical secrets did Raquel preserve within these opulent walls? The answer lies within the intricate details of this captivating portrait, waiting to be uncovered by those who dare to look closer.

Stop 2: Violes d'amour by Joannes Florenus Guidanti (Bologne, 1717)

Address: Palais Lascaris, 15 Rue Droite, 06300 Nice, France

As you gaze upon the Violes d'amour, you are transported back to the year 1717, a time when clandestine concerts echoed through the grand halls of the Palais. This exquisite instrument by Joannes Florenus Guidanti stands as a testament to the artistry of its era. Crafted with precision and care, it boasts a unique design that captivates the eye and stirs the soul. The Violes d'amour is a masterpiece of musical instrument making, featuring a delicate interplay of wood and strings. Its rich tones and resonant sound speak to the skill of Guidanti, a renowned maker from Bologne. The instrument's curved body, adorned with ornate carvings and inlays, showcases the artisan's attention to detail and dedication to his craft. Historically, the Violes d'amour holds a special place in the musical traditions of the time. Believed to have been played during intimate gatherings and lavish performances, it embodies the elegance and sophistication of the Baroque period. Its presence in the collection at Palais Lascaris highlights the significance of music in the aristocratic circles of 18th-century Europe. As you admire this rare instrument, consider the melodies that once emanated from its strings, filling the halls of the Palais with music and enchantment. The Violes d'amour by Joannes Florenus Guidanti invites you to explore the intersection of art, history, and music, offering a glimpse into a bygone era of musical excellence. How did the melodies of Violes d'amour shape the legacy of the Palais Lascaris? This question lingers in the air, inviting you to delve deeper into the history and significance of this remarkable instrument.
