Step-by-Step Audio Guided Tour: Musee Matisse, Nice, France - Museum Tour
Tour-Category: museum

Stop 1: Nymphe dans la forêt

Address: 164 Avenue des Arènes de Cimiez, 06000 Nice, France

Coordinates: 43.7196, 7.2755

Museum Information: Closed on Tuesday. 10:00–17:00 (1 Nov–31 Mar); 10:00–18:00 (1 Apr–31 Oct). €12; free for Métropole residents

You are about to embark on a riveting journey through the interconnected chapters of Henri Matisse's Legacy and Donations at the Musée Matisse. The story begins with the inauguration of the museum in 1963 within the historic Villa des Arènes, a seventeenth-century gem nestled in the Cimiez neighborhood of Nice. As you explore, you will uncover how Matisse's artistic essence is woven into his generous contributions, starting with the enchanting Nymphe dans la forêt. Delve deeper to witness the storm of transformation that mirrors Matisse's own tumultuous yet fruitful years in Nice, culminating in the museum's expansion in 1989. Each stop along this immersive journey reveals a different facet of Matisse's enduring legacy, inviting you to unravel the mysteries of his artistic evolution and philanthropic impact.

In this stunning work of art, created between 1936 and 1938, Matisse transports us to a serene forest setting where a nude nymph encounters a satyr. The composition is a departure from Matisse's usual style, showcasing his versatility and willingness to explore new themes. The painting, executed in oil on canvas, exudes a sense of mystery and allure, inviting viewers to delve into the narrative unfolding before them. Matisse's masterful use of color and light creates a dream-like atmosphere, blurring the lines between reality and fantasy. One striking aspect of "Nymphe dans la forêt" is Matisse's choice to depict the delicate balance between innocence and temptation, as symbolized by the interaction between the nymph and the satyr. This juxtaposition invites contemplation on themes of desire, nature, and the duality of human emotions. Contextually, this artwork represents a pivotal moment in Matisse's career, where he embraced a more symbolic and evocative approach to his subject matter. The painting not only stands as a testament to Matisse's artistic evolution but also offers a glimpse into the artist's deep connection to mythology and the natural world. As you immerse yourself in the mesmerizing world of "Nymphe dans la forêt," consider how Matisse's artistic philosophy is interwoven into this donation, laying the foundation for the remarkable collection at Musee Matisse. This piece is just the beginning of Matisse's legacy within the museum — a legacy that continues to inspire and captivate visitors to this day.

Stop 2: Tempête à Nice

Address: 164 Avenue des Arènes de Cimiez, 06000 Nice, France

As you stand before "Tempête à Nice," created by Henri Matisse between 1919 and 1920, you are transported to a moment frozen in time. The painting depicts the chaotic scene of a storm along the seaside in Nice, as observed from a hotel balcony. Matisse's skillful brushwork brings to life the swirling clouds, turbulent sea, and bending palm trees, all rendered in vivid hues that evoke the energy of the tempest. The use of bold, expressive strokes conveys the raw power and intensity of nature's fury, contrasting with the serene setting of the coastal town. One noteworthy aspect of this artwork is Matisse's choice of color palette, where vibrant blues and greens clash with darker tones to create a sense of impending danger and unease. The dynamic composition and exaggerated forms enhance the dramatic impact of the storm, immersing the viewer in the atmospheric tension of the scene. Understanding the historical context of Matisse's time in Nice adds depth to the interpretation of "Tempête à Nice." During this period, Matisse experienced a turbulent yet creatively fruitful phase in his career, marked by personal challenges and artistic exploration. The storm in the painting can be seen as a metaphor for the artist's own tumultuous years in the city, reflecting the internal conflicts and external influences that shaped his work. This masterpiece is a testament to Matisse's ability to capture the essence of a moment and translate it into a powerful visual narrative. As you contemplate the swirling energy of the storm captured on canvas, you are invited to appreciate the artist's skill in conveying the emotional intensity of nature's forces. "Tempête à Nice" stands as a poignant reminder of Matisse's legacy and his enduring impact on the world of art. End your exploration of this captivating artwork with a newfound appreciation for Matisse's ability to infuse his paintings with emotion and depth, transcending mere representation to evoke a profound sense of awe.



From Nymphe dans la forêt to Tempête à Nice, you have followed the thread of Henri Matisse's Legacy and Donations. In 1989, the archaeological museum was moved to the nearby ancient site of the city, allowing the Musée Matisse to be expanded.
