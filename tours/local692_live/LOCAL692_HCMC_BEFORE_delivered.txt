Stop 1: Vườn xuân Trung Nam Bắc

Coordinates: 10.7741, 106.6953

Orientation: You are about to explore the Ho Chi Minh City Museum of Fine Arts in Vietnam. Your first stop is Vườn xuân Trung Nam Bắc. From Vườn xuân Trung Nam Bắc, you can admire a spot where you can take in its full expanse. Its monumental size, stretching 540cm by 200cm, commands your gaze, allowing you to appreciate the intricate details of this lacquer masterpiece.

Created by Nguyễn Gia Trí, this painting is a magnificent example of lacquer art, using multiple layers of lacquer paint to create depth and vibrancy. The work portrays a harmonious garden scene, symbolizing the unity of Vietnam's North, Central, and South regions. By the time he began Vườn xuân Trung Nam Bắc, Nguyễn Gia Trí was already celebrated for his mastery of the medium, showcasing not only his technical skill but also his deep connection to Vietnam's cultural landscape. The rich hues and detailed textures invite viewers to explore the cultural and historical narratives embedded within the artwork.

Stop 2: Thiếu nữ bên hoa huệ

Coordinates: 10.7741, 106.6953

Orientation: Stand directly in front of "Thiếu nữ bên hoa huệ" to fully appreciate the delicate interplay of light and shadow in the oil paints, revealing subtle details in the young woman's expression and the lilies she holds.

In 1943, Tô Ngọc Vân completed his oil masterpiece, "Thiếu nữ bên hoa huệ," capturing the serene presence of a young woman beside a lily. This painting became emblematic of his stature as a prominent figure in Vietnamese art. Unlike the vibrant celebration of regional unity found in "Vườn xuân Trung Nam Bắc," which you observed earlier, this work offers an introspective moment. The lily, traditionally a symbol of purity, reinforces this feeling and aligns with Vân's vision of showcasing the inherent dignity and resilience of Vietnamese women.

Vân served as a faculty member at the École Supérieure des Beaux-Arts de l'Indochine in Hanoi, where he influenced a generation of artists by merging traditional Vietnamese aesthetics with modern techniques—a principle clearly evident in this painting. During the 1940s, he produced some of his most notable works, even as Vietnam faced significant historical upheavals. Later, the painting entered a period of uncertainty, with its precise location becoming unclear. The museum also holds numerous Champa and Óc Eo relics, providing a historical backdrop that adds depth to the cultural narrative expressed through this artwork.

Continue to Thanh niên thành đồng.

Stop 3: Thanh niên thành đồng

Coordinates: 10.7741, 106.6953

Orientation: Stand back a few steps and to the right of Nguyễn Sáng's Thanh niên thành đồng to grasp its depth and detail. Notice the sheen of the lacquer contrasting with the sturdy wood behind it, evoking the resilience and determination of Southern youth, which the painting so vividly captures.

In 1978, Nguyễn Sáng, a leading figure in Vietnamese art, completed Thanh niên thành đồng. This lacquer painting vividly portrays the spirit of Southern youth during a pivotal era in Vietnamese history. Sáng’s meticulous use of traditional lacquer techniques reveals his commitment to preserving cultural heritage while addressing contemporary themes. The choice of lacquer is significant—Sáng specialized in this medium, resulting in artwork rich in depth and texture. Each figure in the composition is layered with care, reflecting not only aesthetic beauty but also a historical narrative of resilience and hope. At the time, Sáng was navigating the complexities of post-war Vietnam, channeling his optimistic vision for the country’s future into his work. Nguyễn Sáng’s painting stands as both a historical document and a hopeful vision, urging us to reflect on the interweaving of tradition and progress in Vietnamese art. This painting is not just a piece of art; it is a conversation with history, richly layered and deeply human.

Directions: Proceed to Sơn nữ.

Stop 4: Sơn nữ

Coordinates: 10.7741, 106.6953

Orientation: Notice the subtle interplay of light and shadow that reveals the intricate detailing of the young girl's facial features, emphasizing the skill with which Lê Thị Lựu has captured her serene expression.

Sơn nữ," or "Mountain Girl," is an oil-on-canvas painting by Lê Thị Lựu that presents a young girl from the mountainous regions with authentic realism, using delicate brushstrokes. Lê Thị Lựu, celebrated as the first Vietnamese woman to graduate from the École des Beaux-Arts de l'Indochine, demonstrates both her mastery of Western technique and her deep appreciation for traditional Vietnamese subjects in this work. During her time at the École des Beaux-Arts, she honed her skills in painting and learned to integrate diverse influences into her art. "Sơn nữ encapsulates her intent to document and celebrate the diverse ethnic identities that contribute to Vietnam's rich cultural tapestry. Alongside her contemporaries, Lê Thị Lựu played a crucial role in bringing Vietnamese art to the international stage. Her paintings, including "Sơn nữ," depict the daily life and beauty of her subjects and enrich the narrative of the cultural intersections she witnessed. The interplay of modernity and tradition in "Sơn nữ" evokes a sense of intimacy and appreciation for Vietnam's multifaceted identity.
