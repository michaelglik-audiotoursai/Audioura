Stop 1: Nature morte aux grenades

Address: 164 Avenue des Arènes de Cimiez, 06000 Nice, France

Coordinates: 43.7196, 7.2755

Type/Specialty: Still Life Paintings

Specific Examples: Visitors will see Matisse's vibrant still life painting featuring pomegranates.

Museum Information: Open daily from 10am to 6pm. Admission fee required.

Orientation: Orientation: As you approach Nature morte aux grenades by Henri Matisse at Musee Matisse in Nice, position yourself slightly to the left, allowing the natural light from the window to illuminate the vibrant colors of this captivating still life.

You are about to embark on a journey through the interconnected chapters of Henri Matisse's artistic evolution in the city of Nice. Each stop reveals a different facet of the transformative connection between the artist and this vibrant locale. From his exploration of color and form inspired by the local landscape to his deepening introspection within the intimate spaces of his studio, Matisse's art reflects the diverse influences he absorbed in Nice. As we navigate through his works, we witness not only the evolution of his creative vision but also the profound impact this city had on his legacy. Join us as we delve into the colorful tapestry of Matisse's world, woven together by the threads of inspiration that bound him to Nice.

Behold the mesmerizing Nature morte aux grenades, painted by Matisse in November 1947 in Vence. This exquisite piece is part of the Intérieurs de Vence series and showcases pomegranates resting on a crimson table against a backdrop of a palm tree-framed window. The composition exudes a sense of tranquility and abundance, inviting you to linger in contemplation.

Matisse's creative process for this work was deeply rooted in his surroundings in Nice, where he found inspiration in the local landscape. The use of bold colors and simplified forms in this painting marks the beginning of his exploration of vibrant color and form, setting the stage for his later iconic works.

This painting was not only a personal expression for Matisse but also became a symbol of promotion for Nice when it was featured on a tourism poster in 1949. The artist's generous gift of this piece to the city in 1953 further solidifies its significance as a cherished part of the cultural heritage of Nice.

As you gaze upon Nature morte aux grenades, notice the delicate brushstrokes that bring the pomegranates to life, the interplay of light and shadow that adds depth to the composition, and the overall harmony that Matisse achieved with his masterful technique. This artwork not only captures a moment in time but also serves as a testament to Matisse's evolving artistic vision and his deep connection to the vibrant energy of Nice.

Immerse yourself in the beauty and symbolism of this stunning still life, and let the rich history and cultural significance of Nature morte aux grenades transport you to a world where art, nature, and harmony converge in perfect balance.

Stop 2: Nature morte à la statuette africaine

Address: 164 Avenue des Arènes de Cimiez, 06000 Nice, France

Type/Specialty: Still Life Paintings

Specific Examples: This exhibit showcases Matisse's still life painting with an African statuette.

Orientation: Nature morte à la statuette africaine, an unfinished painting by Henri Matisse, captures the essence of the artist's fascination with African art. Completed between 1907-1912, this still life stands out with its uncommon white background, a departure from Matisse's typical vibrant palettes. The painting features a Vili statuette placed amidst three vases and a glass on a delicate pink surface, creating a captivating contrast against the neutral background.

Matisse's creative process for this piece reflects his experimentation with form and composition, influenced by the diverse cultural influences he absorbed in Nice. The use of the white background, unusual for Matisse, adds a sense of purity and simplicity to the composition, drawing attention to the details and symbolism within the artwork.

Displayed as part of the museum's collection, Nature morte à la statuette africaine offers visitors a glimpse into Matisse's evolving artistic identity and his exploration of African art's impact on his work. The painting, donated by Barbara Duthuit in 2025, serves as a testament to Matisse's artistic versatility and willingness to push boundaries in his artistic expression.

As you observe this piece, take note of the subtle interplay between the statuette and the surrounding objects, each carefully positioned to create a harmonious yet intriguing composition. The unfinished nature of the painting adds a sense of mystery, inviting viewers to ponder the artist's intentions and the potential narrative behind the scene depicted. Nature morte à la statuette africaine is a visual feast for those interested in Matisse's artistic journey and the cultural influences that shaped his work.

Stop 3: Nymphe dans la forêt

Address: 164 Avenue des Arènes de Cimiez, 06000 Nice, France

Type/Specialty: Figurative Art

Specific Examples: Visitors will experience Matisse's depiction of a nymph in the forest.

Orientation: Orientation: To fully appreciate Nymphe dans la forêt in the Musee Matisse, position yourself at a slight angle to the left of the painting, allowing the soft light from the window to gently illuminate the scene, enhancing the ethereal quality of the artwork.

Mesmerizing in its tranquil beauty, Nymphe dans la forêt transports viewers to a mythical realm where Henri Matisse masterfully captures a serene moment in nature. Created between 1936 and 1938, this oil on canvas painting depicts a nude nymph enveloped by the lush foliage of a forest, her form exuding a sense of graceful vulnerability. As she is approached by a satyr, the tension between innocence and desire is palpable, adding layers of complexity to the narrative.

Matisse's creative process for this piece was deeply rooted in his love for the vibrant landscapes of Nice, where he found inspiration in the harmonious coexistence of light and nature. The artist's brushstrokes, ranging from delicate to bold, skillfully render the interplay of shadows and highlights, infusing the scene with a sense of movement and life.

Nymphe dans la forêt holds a special place within the Musee Matisse's collection, offering a profound exploration of the human experience in relation to the natural world. This work, currently on deposit at the museum in Nice, Alpes-Maritimes, invites visitors to ponder the intricate connections between desire, beauty, and the untamed wilderness.

As you gaze upon this enchanting painting, consider how Matisse's fascination with the harmony of nature influenced his evolving artistic style. Nymphe dans la forêt stands as a testament to the artist's ability to capture fleeting moments of emotion and beauty, inviting us to reflect on our own place within the grand tapestry of life.

Matisse's creative process shines through in the juxtaposition of the woman's flesh tones against the vibrant pink of her blouse, creating a harmonious balance of hues. The presence of the plant introduces a natural element into the interior setting, symbolizing growth and vitality within the enclosed space.

This painting reflects Matisse's exploration of the nude form and his mastery of capturing the essence of human grace and beauty. The artist's deep connection to his studio in Nice resonates in the intimate portrayal of the figure, evoking a sense of tranquility and introspection.

As you gaze upon "Nu dans un fauteuil, plante verte," consider how Matisse's use of the nude form conveys a profound sense of intimacy and personal reflection. This work, displayed within the Musee Matisse in Nice, stands as a testament to the artist's ability to infuse everyday scenes with a transcendent quality.

Stop 5: Nu renversé, étendu sur le dos

Address: 164 Avenue des Arènes de Cimiez, 06000 Nice, France

Type/Specialty: Figurative Art

Specific Examples: Visitors will see Matisse's artwork depicting a nude figure lying on their back.

Orientation: Henri Matisse's circa 1946 oil on canvas sketch, Nu renversé, étendu sur le dos, offers a poignant exploration of the human form and vulnerability. In this piece, a nude woman lies on her back, her head tilted towards the lower right corner of the composition. The artwork's unfinished state, left in a sketch form, provides a unique glimpse into Matisse's creative process, showcasing his raw and spontaneous expression.

The simplicity of the composition belies its emotional depth. Matisse's bold brushwork captures the essence of the figure with a sense of intimacy and sensitivity. The muted tones and subtle shading create a mood of contemplation and introspection, inviting viewers to ponder the vulnerability and beauty of the human form.

This work not only showcases Matisse's daring exploration of the human figure but also parallels the museum's expansion to feature more of his unconventional and daring works. Nu renversé, étendu sur le dos stands as a testament to Matisse's ability to convey profound emotion and depth through minimalistic yet powerful compositions.

As you gaze upon this piece, notice the subtle details that speak volumes about the artist's vision. The way the figure's form blends into the background, the gentle curve of her body, and the expressive tilt of her head all contribute to the overall sense of vulnerability and grace conveyed in this captivating artwork.

Nu renversé, étendu sur le dos is a gem in the Musee Matisse's collection, offering a glimpse into Matisse's artistic process and his ability to capture the essence of the human experience with simplicity and depth. It is a must-see for those seeking to delve into the emotional core of Matisse's work and the power of vulnerability in art.

Stop 6: Lectrice à la table jaune

Address: 164 Avenue des Arènes de Cimiez, 06000 Nice, France

Type/Specialty: Figurative Art

Specific Examples: This exhibit features Matisse's painting of a reader at a yellow table.

Orientation: In 1944, Henri Matisse created this oil on canvas, depicting a serene blonde woman engrossed in reading an illustrated book at a yellow table. What sets this scene apart are the unexpected elements – two grenades resting alongside a delicate vase of flowers and a wine glass. This juxtaposition of peaceful reading and potential danger invites contemplation on the fragility of existence during wartime.

The model for this painting was Annelies Nelck, lending a sense of intimacy to the composition. Matisse's creative process shines through in his expert use of color and light, with the yellow table exuding warmth and the woman bathed in soft, natural light. Each object in the painting is meticulously rendered, inviting you to linger and explore the intricate symbolism woven throughout.

Placed within the broader context of Musee Matisse, Lectrice à la table jaune stands as a testament to Matisse's ability to capture moments of quiet reflection amidst tumultuous times. The museum's reflective redesigns over the years echo the introspective nature of this piece, drawing parallels between the artist's creative journey and the museum's evolving narrative.

As you gaze upon this work, consider how Matisse's contemplative periods influenced his later creations. The inclusion of the grenades sparks curiosity and prompts deeper reflection on the complexities of human experience. Allow yourself to be transported into Matisse's world, where beauty and danger coexist in a delicate balance, inviting you to ponder the mysteries of life and art.

Stop 7: Matisse dans la collection Nahmad

Address: 164 Avenue des Arènes de Cimiez, 06000 Nice, France

Type/Specialty: Various Works by Matisse

Specific Examples: Visitors will see a collection of Matisse's artworks from the Nahmad collection.

Orientation: Immerse yourself in the world of Matisse with this unique collection that showcases his multifaceted talent beyond painting. Did you know that Matisse was not only a painter but also a skilled printmaker and sculptor? This exhibit offers a glimpse into this lesser-known aspect of his creative repertoire.

At the heart of the collection is a series of three drawings that exemplify Matisse's mastery of line and form. One drawing, Académie, nu de femme (1900), captures the essence of the female form with a delicate balance of light and shadow. Another, Vase de fleurs (1928), blooms with intricate floral details that seem to dance off the page. The third piece, Visage, main devant la bouche, invites you to contemplate the interplay of gestures and expressions frozen in time.

These drawings not only showcase Matisse's technical skill but also offer a glimpse into his creative process and exploration of different mediums. As you admire these works, consider how they fit into the broader context of the museum's collection and the evolution of Matisse's artistic vision.

This collection serves as a testament to Matisse's enduring influence and the depth of his artistic legacy. Explore these drawings with a sense of fascination and curiosity, and ponder how Matisse's work transcends cultural boundaries, much like Nice itself has become a cultural hub through its association with his legacy.

Stop 8: Figure à l'ombrelle

Address: 164 Avenue des Arènes de Cimiez, 06000 Nice, France

Type/Specialty: Figurative Art

Specific Examples: This exhibit showcases Matisse's painting featuring a figure with an umbrella.

Orientation: In 1905, in the quaint town of Collioure, Pyrénées-Orientales, Henri Matisse created Figure à l'ombrelle, a stunning example of his experimentation with the pointillist technique. The canvas vibrates with energy as Matisse expertly applied small, distinct dots of color to capture the essence of light and form. The figure, elegantly poised under a parasol, exudes a sense of tranquility amidst a background of rich hues and dynamic patterns.

This work is a testament to Matisse's mastery during his peak years in Nice, where he found inspiration in the Mediterranean light and landscape. The dramatic interplay of light and shadow in Figure à l'ombrelle encapsulates the artist's bold use of color and innovative approach to composition. The painting not only reflects Matisse's creative process but also invites viewers to contemplate the beauty and complexity of everyday moments.

Notice how the figure's shadow, curves, and the interplay between the striped wall and the languid pose create a captivating visual dialogue. Explore the layers of color and texture that Matisse skillfully weaves together, inviting you to immerse yourself in the world he has created.

As you gaze upon Figure à l'ombrelle, consider the legacy that Matisse left behind in Nice and the lasting impact of his revolutionary artistic vision. Marvel at the fusion of technique and emotion in this painting, a true gem in the Musee Matisse collection.

Stop 9: Blue Nude IV

Address: 164 Avenue des Arènes de Cimiez, 06000 Nice, France

Type/Specialty: Figurative Art

Specific Examples: Visitors will see Matisse's famous Blue Nude IV painting.

Orientation: Henri Matisse's Blue Nude IV stands as an emblem of the artist's relentless innovation and creative spirit. This iconic work, part of his renowned Blue Nudes series, showcases a nude figure in a contemplative pose, rendered with a striking simplicity that belies its profound complexity. Matisse's physical challenges after surgery led him to explore a new artistic approach, cutting and arranging hand-painted paper sheets to create a harmonious composition that exudes a sense of tranquility and introspection.

In this piece, the figure's elongated form is bathed in a luminous cerulean blue, evoking a sense of calm and serenity. The deliberate use of negative space and the fluid, organic lines enhance the figure's graceful presence, inviting contemplation and reflection. The subtle gradations of blue, from deep indigo to ethereal azure, create a sense of depth and movement within the composition.

As you gaze upon Blue Nude IV, consider Matisse's revolutionary technique of using cut-outs as independent artworks, challenging traditional notions of artistic creation. This exploration of form and color, embodied in the Blue Nudes series, represents a pinnacle of Matisse's artistic evolution and is a testament to his unwavering dedication to pushing the boundaries of artistic expression.

Embedded within the broader context of Musee Matisse, this masterpiece symbolizes the museum's celebration of Matisse's legacy and ongoing commitment to preserving and showcasing the artist's groundbreaking contributions to the world of art. Stop 10: Still Life

Address: 164 Avenue des Arènes de Cimiez, 06000 Nice, France

Type/Specialty: Still Life Paintings

Specific Examples: This exhibit showcases various still life paintings by Matisse.

Orientation: In this enchanting still life, Matisse's "Nature morte aux grenades" transports you to a world where simplicity meets sophistication. The composition radiates a sense of quiet contemplation, inviting you to linger and unravel its secrets. The artist's deliberate brushstrokes breathe life into the scene, capturing the essence of a fleeting moment frozen in time.

Against a backdrop of muted earth tones, a porcelain bowl overflows with luscious pomegranates, their ruby-red seeds glistening in the soft glow. The play of light on the textured surface of the fruit creates a mesmerizing dance of shadows, infusing the painting with a sense of depth and richness.

Matisse's meticulous attention to detail is evident in the delicate rendering of each seed and the subtle variations in color that bring the composition to life. The contrast between the smooth porcelain and the rough skin of the pomegranates adds a tactile quality to the work, inviting you to reach out and touch the velvety fruit.

This piece, nestled within the museum's collection, serves as a poignant reminder of Matisse's mastery in capturing the beauty of everyday objects. It encapsulates the essence of still life painting, celebrating the ordinary in an extraordinary way. So, take a moment to savor the quiet elegance of "Nature morte aux grenades" and let its understated charm wash over you like a whispered secret shared among friends.
