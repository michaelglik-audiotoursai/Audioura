Step-by-Step Audio Guided Tour: Palais Lascaris, Nice - Museum Tour
Tour-Category: museum

Stop 1: Raquel

Address: 11 Rue Pairolière, 06300 Nice, France

Coordinates: 43.69671, 7.27192

Museum Information: 14:00–21:00

You are about to embark on a journey through the hallowed halls of Palais Lascaris, a testament to 17th-century artistry and innovation. Completed in the first half of the seventeenth century and owned by the Vintimille-Lascaris family until 1802, this architectural gem holds the secrets of music and craftsmanship within its walls. As you delve into the chapters of this immersive experience, you will uncover the enigmatic figure of Raquel, a patron of the arts commissioning groundbreaking pieces. The crescendo awaits as you discover the Violes gambe by William Turner, a masterpiece blending English craftsmanship with the artistic legacy of Palais Lascaris. Step into history as the symphony of the past echoes through each carefully restored room, revealing the intricate tapestry of creativity woven into the very fabric of this magnificent palace.

Description:
"Raquel" mesmerizes viewers with its exquisite depiction of a mysterious figure associated with the Lascaris family. The wooden panel, emanating a warm, honeyed hue, showcases a woman elegantly adorned in flowing robes, her expression enigmatic yet alluring. As your eyes trace the delicate lines and intricate patterns carved into the wood, you can't help but marvel at the skill of the artisan who brought Raquel to life. One of the most striking features of this artwork is the meticulous attention to detail in the carving technique. Each fold of the woman's garment, every curl of her hair, and the subtle play of light and shadow on her face reveal the artist's mastery of form and texture. The choice of wood as the medium adds a tactile dimension to the piece, inviting contemplation of the artist's skill in transforming a solid block into a work of art that seems to breathe with life. To fully appreciate "Raquel," one must consider the historical context of the Lascaris family and their patronage of the arts. Raquel is believed to have been a significant figure in this context, likely a patron who commissioned pieces like this panel, pushing the boundaries of contemporary craftsmanship. The panel not only reflects the artistic sensibilities of its time but also hints at the intriguing stories and relationships that shaped the cultural landscape of Nice. As you stand before "Raquel," you can't help but wonder about the groundbreaking instrument that might have captured Raquel's imagination. The panel serves as a portal to a bygone era, where art and mystery intertwine to create a captivating narrative that continues to intrigue visitors to this day.

Stop 2: Violes gambe by William Turner (Londres, 1652)

Address: 15 Rue Droite, 06300 Nice, France

Description:
Crafted in London in 1652, the Violes gambe by William Turner stands as a testament to the pinnacle of 17th-century musical instrument design. This viola da gamba showcases the meticulous craftsmanship of Turner, with its elegantly curved body made of rich, dark wood, exquisitely carved with floral motifs that speak to the artistic patronage of the era. The strings, when plucked, resonate with a hauntingly beautiful tone that echoes through the halls of the Palais Lascaris. One striking feature of this instrument is Turner's choice of materials, using fine woods and precise joinery techniques to create a harmonious blend of form and function. The intricate inlays and delicate carvings on the Violes gambe not only add to its aesthetic appeal but also enhance the acoustics, allowing for a more resonant sound when played. Historically, this viola da gamba represents a fusion of musical traditions, reflecting the cultural exchange and influence between England and continental Europe during the 17th century. The craftsmanship of Turner's instrument influenced subsequent generations of luthiers, shaping the development of stringed instruments for centuries to come. As you admire the Violes gambe by William Turner, consider how this exceptional piece of craftsmanship embodies the artistry and innovation of its time.
