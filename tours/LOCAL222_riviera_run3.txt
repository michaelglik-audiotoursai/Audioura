Stop 1: Cap d'Antibes Coastal Path

Address: Sentier du Littoral, 06160 Cap d'Antibes, France

Coordinates: 43.5412, 7.1234

Type/Specialty: Coastal Path

Specific Examples: Stunning views of the Mediterranean Sea, historic lighthouses, beautiful beaches

Orientation: Start at the main parking lot near the entrance of the coastal path and head south along the marked trail towards the sea. Enjoy the sea breeze as you cycle along the path. As you stand at the starting point of the Cap d'Antibes Coastal Path, the shimmering Mediterranean Sea stretches out before you, inviting you to embark on a journey along the stunning coastline. The gentle sound of waves lapping against the shore sets the tranquil tone for your exploration of this historic and picturesque location.

You are about to embark on a journey through the French Riviera, where the breathtaking vistas belie a storied past of piracy and artistic inspiration. As you traverse from the Cap d'Antibes Coastal Path to the Voie Verte du Littoral Varois, each step unravels a chapter of transformation—from the footprints of fishermen and smugglers to echoes of creativity, conflict, and change. Feel the breeze once felt by Claude Monet as he painted these very cliffs, and touch the ancient stones of the old railway line, a symbol of wartime resilience turned peaceful cycling path. The secrets of this sun-kissed paradise are waiting to be unveiled beneath its radiant exterior, revealing a tapestry of history waiting to be explored.

The Cap d'Antibes Coastal Path, also known as the sentier Littoral or sentier Tire-poil, stretches almost 3.5 kilometers from plage de la Garoupe to the rugged cliffs of Cap d'Antibes, near Villa Eilenroc. The path showcases stunning views of azure waters and dramatic cliffs that have long inspired artists and writers. Ponder the connection to F. Scott Fitzgerald's novel "Tender Is the Night," set on the French Riviera during the Jazz Age. Reflect on the parallels between the protagonist's descent and the author's own struggles, adding a layer of literary history to this idyllic coastal setting. The warm Mediterranean breeze carries whispers of history intertwined with the natural beauty of the surroundings. The lush greenery, the scent of salt in the air, and the distant calls of seagulls create a sensory tapestry that immerses visitors in the essence of the French Riviera. This coastal path offers a serene escape into nature and serves as a living link to the cultural heritage of Antibes. The region's rich history is showcased through landmarks like the Hôtel du Cap-Eden-Roc and the Grand-Hôtel du Cap-Ferrat, renowned for their exclusivity and elegance. As you round the next bend, imagine the echoes of the past guiding you towards another hidden gem along the coast, where history and nature converge in perfect harmony.

Directions: Head northeast on the Cap d'Antibes Coastal Path until you reach Avenue de l'Esterel. Turn right onto Avenue de l'Esterel and continue until you reach Voie Verte du Littoral Varois. You'll know you're in the right place when you see the sign for the cycling path along the Var coast.
