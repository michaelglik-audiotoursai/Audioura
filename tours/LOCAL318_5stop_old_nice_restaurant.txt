Acchiardo, a cornerstone of Niçoise tradition, was established in 1927 by Madalin Acchiardo, a widow who entered the restaurant business after her husband, Giuseppe, passed away in 1920. The restaurant's charm lies in its steadfast commitment to Niçoise cuisine, offering dishes like daube, a Provençal beef stew simmered slowly in wine, echoing the rustic comforts of regional cooking.
