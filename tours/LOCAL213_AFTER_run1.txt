Stop 1: Cap d'Antibes

Address: Cap d'Antibes, 06160 Antibes, France

Coordinates: 43.5421, 7.1229

Type/Specialty: Scenic coastal area

Specific Examples: Beautiful beaches, luxury villas, stunning views of the Mediterranean Sea

Orientation: Start cycling southeast on the main road, Avenue du Cap d'Antibes, enjoy the sea breeze along the coast. As you arrive at Cap d'Antibes, the salty tang of the Mediterranean Sea fills the air, mingling with the fragrant scent of pine trees. Find yourself at the edge of the peninsula, where the azure waters stretch out endlessly before you under the warm French Riviera sun.

You are about to embark on a journey through the French Riviera, where the opulence of today intertwines with the echoes of a storied past. Hidden pathways on Cap d'Antibes once whispered the secrets of F. Scott Fitzgerald and Picasso, their creative spirits lingering in the sea breeze, while atop Mont Boron, a strategic military lookout now reveals a panoramic vista of the Riviera's transformation from a war-torn boundary to a haven of peace. What secrets lie beneath the sun-drenched glamour, waiting to be unveiled by those who venture off the beaten path? The French Riviera beckons you to explore its chapters, each revealing a different facet of this captivating tapestry where luxury and history intertwine, inviting you to uncover the forgotten tales that define these shores.

Cap d'Antibes holds secrets within its rugged beauty. In January 1888, renowned artist Claude Monet sought inspiration here, staying at the Château de la Pinède on the advice of his friend Guy de Maupassant. The coastal path known as "Tire-Poil" winds along the cap, offering breathtaking views of the sea and surrounding landscapes. From the Garoupe Beach parking lot to Villa Eilenroc, this 2.7 km trail reveals the natural splendor and historic landmarks that dot the coastline. The winding paths and rocky outcrops of Cap d'Antibes once inspired F. Scott Fitzgerald and Picasso, who sought solace in its secluded corners. Their creative spirits linger in the gentle sea breeze, infusing the atmosphere with artistic energy from days gone by. The connection to the tour's theme of community rhythms and hidden stories is palpable here at Cap d'Antibes. The cape's unique geography, flanked by the sea and historical buildings, offers a glimpse into the artistic retreats of the past. With each step along the coastal path, you tread in the footsteps of visionaries who sought refuge and inspiration in this picturesque setting. As you pedal around the peninsula, uncover the untold tales of creativity hidden in the pathways of Cap d'Antibes.

Directions: As you leave the beautiful Cap d'Antibes, head towards the coastline and follow the scenic Promenade des Anglais towards Nice. Once you reach Nice, make your way up towards Mont Boron, enjoying the stunning views of the Mediterranean Sea along the way. Look out for the Parc du Mont Boron as you approach your destination.

Stop 2: Mont Boron

Address: 06300 Nice, France

Coordinates: 43.6961, 7.287

Type/Specialty: Hilltop park

Specific Examples: Panoramic views of Nice, lush greenery, historic fortifications

Orientation: As you stand atop Mont Boron, a hill in the southeastern part of Nice, in the Alpes-Maritimes department, with its peak reaching 191.3 meters, the panoramic vista before you offers a glimpse into the rich tapestry of history that has unfolded along the French Riviera. From this strategic military lookout point, you can witness the transformation of the region from a war-torn boundary to a haven of peace.

The Baie des Anges, known as the Bay of Angels, stretches between Antibes to the west and Nice to the east, forming a picturesque backdrop to the landscape. This bay of the Mediterranean Sea is bordered by significant landmarks, such as Nice Côte d’Azur airport, the iconic Promenade des Anglais, Lympia port, and the prominent Mont Boron. History intertwines with myth and legend in this region, as Christian mythology tells of angels bringing the boat containing the martyr body of Saint Réparate to these shores in the 3rd century. Local traditions also speak of compassionate angels guiding Adam and Eve to these very shores after their expulsion from paradise. The name "Baie des Anges" is not merely a geographical designation but also a reflection of local folklore, where fishermen encountered angelsharks in their nets, inspiring the bay's celestial moniker. The poet Alexis de Jussieu's work "Adam et Ève" further romanticizes the connection between angels and the bay, deepening the mystical allure of the area. Mont Boron, with its commanding presence, serves as a silent witness to the centuries of history that have shaped the French Riviera. The hill's elevation offers a unique perspective, connecting visitors with the region's past as they gaze upon the azure waters and rugged coastline below. As you take in this awe-inspiring view, consider the resilience and evolution that define the Riviera's story — a narrative of adaptation and transformation over time.
