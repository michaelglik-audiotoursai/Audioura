Tour-Category: walking

Stop 1: Cap d'Antibes

Address: Cap d'Antibes, 06160 Antibes, France

Coordinates: 43.5411, 7.1225

Type/Specialty: Scenic coastal area

Specific Examples: Beautiful views of the Mediterranean Sea, historic lighthouse, luxury villas

Orientation: Start at Antibes train station, head southwest on Avenue Robert Soleau, continue onto Avenue de la Libération, then follow signs to Cap d'Antibes. Enjoy the coastal breeze as you cycle along the waterfront. As you arrive at Cap d'Antibes, the gentle sea breeze carries the scent of saltwater and pine, mingling with the sound of waves crashing against the rugged coastline. Find yourself at the edge of the peninsula, near the iconic Villa Eilenroc, offering a stunning view of the Mediterranean Sea and the distant horizon.

You are about to embark on a journey through the French Riviera, a tapestry of opulent lifestyles and artistic legacies. The Chapelle de la Garoupe, nestled among luxurious villas, offers a breathtaking view over the sparkling waters—a favorite spot of Fitzgerald and Picasso. Beyond the bustling port, the quiet Annonciade Museum houses masterpieces by the artists who sought refuge and inspiration here. As you wander these sun-drenched paths, hidden stories of glamour and artistic rebellion unfold, revealing the shimmering allure that captivated the hearts and minds of those who once walked these same streets. Join us as we unravel the chapters of forgotten grandeur and creative revolutions that lie beneath the serene beauty of the coastline, waiting to be discovered.

Claude Monet explored Cap d'Antibes in 1888 during his artistic journey through the south of France. This stretch of land served as a retreat for the renowned painter, who sought inspiration among the coastal scenery and crystalline waters. Monet's presence at Cap d'Antibes resonates through the landscape, where the Tire-Poil coastal path winds its way from the Garoupe Beach parking lot to Villa Eilenroc, offering a 2.7 km trail with panoramic views of the Lerins Islands and the Mercantour Mountains. Walk alongside the imposing walls of the Garoupe and Croë castles in the rich tapestry of the Cap d'Antibes. Witness the ancient Notre Dame de Bon Port chapel and the commanding Garoupe lighthouse perched atop the highest point. The Abri de l'Olivette, a sheltered port for traditional local boats, adds a touch of maritime charm to the western coast. Stand amidst the history and natural beauty of Cap d'Antibes, where rugged rocks beneath your feet bear witness to centuries of coastal allure. As you journey along the French Riviera, let the whispers of the past illuminate new adventures and discoveries, weaving into the rich fabric of this region's cultural heritage.

Directions: Hey there! To get from Cap d'Antibes to Saint-Tropez, head towards the main road and look for signs pointing to the A8 highway. Follow the signs to Nice, then continue on the highway towards Saint-Tropez. Once you reach Saint-Tropez, you'll see the beautiful harbor and vibrant cafes welcoming you to this charming coastal town. Enjoy your walk!

Stop 2: Saint-Tropez

Address: 83990 Saint-Tropez, France

Coordinates: 43.2702, 6.6405

Type/Specialty: Chic coastal town

Specific Examples: Vibrant harbor, pastel-colored houses, trendy cafes

Orientation: As you approach Saint-Tropez on your French Riviera cycling tour, you'll discover a picturesque coastal town nestled between Nice and Marseille. The azure waters of the Mediterranean glisten under the sun, while vibrant streets lined with charming cafes and boutiques beckon you to explore. Look out for the iconic harbor teeming with luxury yachts, a testament to the town's glamorous allure.

Amidst the bustling ambiance of Saint-Tropez, a tranquil haven awaits. The Annonciade Museum stands as a refuge for art enthusiasts, showcasing masterpieces by renowned artists who sought solace and inspiration in this very town. The serene corner has had a profound impact on the creative minds that once wandered its streets. Saint-Tropez has a rich history, once a military stronghold and fishing village that witnessed pivotal moments like its liberation during World War II. The cobblestone streets hold echoes of the past, with the scent of sea salt lingering in the air. This stop on your journey serves as a poignant reminder of the enduring influence of art and culture in shaping the identity of a place. As you continue your exploration through the enchanting streets of Saint-Tropez, ponder the hidden stories waiting to be uncovered in every corner. The past whispers its secrets to those who listen, inviting you to delve deeper into the layers of history woven into the fabric of this captivating town.
