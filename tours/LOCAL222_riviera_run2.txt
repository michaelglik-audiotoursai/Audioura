Stop 1: Cap d'Antibes

Address: Cap d'Antibes, 06160 Antibes, France

Coordinates: 43.5411, 7.1236

Type/Specialty: Scenic coastal area

Specific Examples: Beautiful views of the Mediterranean Sea, luxury villas, sandy beaches

Orientation: Start cycling southwest on Avenue de la Garoupe towards Cap d'Antibes. Enjoy the sea breeze as you pass by stunning villas. As you arrive at Cap d'Antibes on your French Riviera cycling tour, take a moment to soak in the salty breeze coming off the Mediterranean Sea. The rhythmic lapping of waves against the rocky shore sets the backdrop for your exploration of this historic cape.

You are about to embark on a journey through the French Riviera, where the opulence of the past intertwines with the raw beauty of nature, revealing a landscape steeped in history and intrigue. As you traverse the scenic routes, you'll uncover the hidden stories beneath the glamorous surface of this famed coastline and rugged gorges. Get ready to peel back the layers of this storied region, where every turn reveals a new facet of the French Riviera's rich tapestry.

Description:
Cap d'Antibes, along with its counterpart Cap Ferrat, stands as a sentinel of elegance on the French Riviera. Named for the town of Antibes, this cape boasts the prestigious Hôtel du Cap-Eden-Roc, a sanctuary for the world's elite seeking respite by the sea. The grandeur of this exclusive enclave is palpable, with each step revealing a new layer of opulence and history. In the 1930s, the American author F. Scott Fitzgerald found inspiration in the tranquil beauty of Cap d'Antibes for his novel "Tender Is the Night." The captivating tale of love, ambition, and despair unfolds against the backdrop of this picturesque coastline, where echoes of the Jazz Age still linger in the sea breeze. The sentier Littoral is a scenic coastal path that stretches nearly 3.5 kilometers from plage de la Garoupe to Cap d'Antibes, offering breathtaking views of the azure waters and lush greenery. Locally known as the sentier Tire-poil, this trail guides you through a tapestry of natural wonders, leading you to the very heart of this iconic cape. Cap d'Antibes, with its storied past and timeless allure, embodies the hidden pathways where artists and writers sought solace and inspiration away from the bustling cities. As you pedal forward, the landscape shifts dramatically, promising tales of natural wonder just ahead, inviting you to uncover the mysteries and beauty of this enchanting coastal gem. Your exploration of Cap d'Antibes reveals hidden stories within the folds of this historic cape, enhancing your journey along the French Riviera.

Directions: As you leave Cap d'Antibes, head towards Antibes city center. Once there, look for signs pointing towards Grasse or Vence as you make your way towards Gorges du Loup. Enjoy the scenic views as you pass through charming villages and lush landscapes on your journey.

Stop 2: Gorges du Loup

Address: Gorges du Loup, 06140 Tourrettes-sur-Loup, France

Coordinates: 43.7163, 6.9892

Type/Specialty: Natural canyon with waterfalls

Specific Examples: Dramatic cliffs, lush greenery, cascading waterfalls

Orientation: As you approach the Gorges du Loup, situated in the captivating Alpes-Maritimes region of France, you will be surrounded by the rugged beauty of the French Riviera. Look for the untamed Loup river winding through the dramatic limestone cliffs, creating a breathtaking natural spectacle that has captivated travelers for centuries.

Description:
Cycling through the ancient trails of Gorges du Loup, you are immersed in the essence of a bygone era. This historical passageway once echoed with the footsteps of merchants and explorers, traversing the rocky terrain with determination and awe. The sheer cliffs tower above you, bearing witness to the passage of time and the resilience of nature. The cool mist rising from the river below carries the whispers of centuries past, inviting introspection and reverence for the untamed spirit of this region. The wild beauty of the Gorges du Loup contrasts with the refined allure of the Riviera, defining this landscape. The rugged terrain and ancient trails reflect a time long gone, while the sophistication of the French Riviera beckons from the horizon, a testament to the enduring allure of this coastal paradise.
