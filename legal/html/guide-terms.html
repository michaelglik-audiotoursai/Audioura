Applies when you price and sell a tour, in addition to the Terms of Service, Content Policy and Privacy Policy. Pricing a tour means accepting these terms — the app asks with a required checkbox and records it.

1. Pricing

  • (a) You set a price from Audioura's price ladder — a fixed set of allowed prices. The picker offers round dollar amounts from $2 to $200, with quick picks at $3, $5, $10, $20 and $50; a typed amount snaps to the nearest step. This is your list price: Apple and Google apply their own fees, taxes and currency conversions, so the price a listener sees may vary by storefront. (Tours priced before the ladder keep their original price.)
  • (b) Listeners buy your tour per language. Each translation is a separate product, listed and priced separately — a listener who buys English does not receive Spanish.
  • (c) One download per purchase. Each purchase pays for one download of your tour in that language. A listener who buys downloads it to their device and then plays it offline, as often as they like, forever. Deleting the tour means buying it again. A listener who loses or replaces a phone can restore their Listen page (paid tours included) with their recovery code at no charge. A download that fails or is interrupted may be retried at no charge.

2. Evaluation and content rating

Every tour is evaluated automatically: (i) an Audioura score — a model reads the tour's text, the same measure for every tour; and (ii) a content rating — General, Mature (18+) or Not listed — decided by a model reading meaning in context, not by a banned-word list. Scores and ratings are automated, best-effort and may be imperfect, and may change after review or after you edit the tour. A Mature tour is shown only to listeners who have turned on "Show 18+ tours" and confirmed they are 18 or older. Not-listed tours are never listed.

3. Review queue

A tour that is not clearly General, or that our systems flag, is held for review and is not listed until it has been reviewed. Being held for review does not guarantee listing, and Audioura may hold a tour for review at any time, including after it has been listed.
