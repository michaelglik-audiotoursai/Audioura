## How refunds work
All Audioura purchases — individual tours and community passes — are made through the **Apple App Store** or **Google Play**. **Apple and Google handle the money and the refunds.** Audioura does not process payments and cannot issue a refund directly.

To request a refund, use the store you bought from:
- **Apple:** request a refund at **reportaproblem.apple.com** (or in your iPhone/iPad purchase history).
- **Google:** request a refund through **Google Play** (play.google.com → account → order history), subject to Google's refund rules.

Each store decides refunds under its own policy and timeframes.

## What happens to a refunded tour
Each tour purchase pays for **one download** of that tour in that language (see the Terms, "What a purchase gives you"). When Apple or Google tells us a tour purchase was refunded:
- If the purchase had **not yet been used to download** the tour, the unlock is removed at the next time your device checks in online, so it can no longer be downloaded.
- A **verified rating or review** you left on that tour (which is "verified" because you had bought it) is **removed** when all of your purchases of that tour are refunded.
- Audio you had already downloaded with a purchase may remain on your device; a refund does not reach onto your device to delete files you already downloaded, but it removes the unused unlock on our side.

## What happens to a refunded pass
When a community pass purchase is refunded:
- Your **community membership ends** at the next online check, and you stop receiving new community audio.
- As with tours, audio you had already downloaded during the active period is on your device; the server does not delete your downloaded files, but your membership and access to **new** content end.

## Questions
Refund decisions are made by Apple/Google. For anything else, contact **info@audioura.com**.
