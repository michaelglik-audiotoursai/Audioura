## 1. All purchases go through Apple or Google
Audioura sells individual audio tours and community passes **exclusively through in-app purchase** via the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google processes your payment, holds your card details, and collects and remits sales tax / VAT where required. **Audioura LLC never receives or stores your card number, and cannot issue a refund directly.**

## 2. How to request a refund
Refunds are requested from, and decided by, **Apple or Google — not by Audioura**. Their refund policies and timeframes apply.

- **Apple App Store:** open **reportaproblem.apple.com**, sign in with your Apple ID, find the Audioura purchase and request a refund. You can also use your purchase history on your iPhone.
- **Google Play:** open **play.google.com** → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history, find the Audioura purchase and request a refund, or use Google Play Help.

If you need a receipt or a purchase date in order to ask, you can find it in your Apple App Store or Google Play purchase history — or write to info@audioura.com and we will help you locate the transaction.

## 3. What happens after a refund is granted
Each tour purchase pays for **one download** of that tour in that language (see the [Terms](/terms), "What a purchase gives you"). Downloading a tour again later is a new purchase; if you lose or replace your phone you can restore your Listen page with your recovery code, without paying again. If Apple or Google grants a refund:

- **(a) Access may be revoked.** Access to that tour or community pass may be revoked. Revocation takes effect at your device's **next online check**, not instantly — Audioura works offline, so your device learns about it the next time it reaches us. If the refunded purchase had **not yet been used to download** the tour, the unlock is removed so it can no longer be downloaded.
- **(b) Counts and ratings may be adjusted.** Any download count, listen count, or verified listener rating associated with that refunded purchase may be adjusted or removed.
- **(c) A refund does not delete files already on your device.** Audio you had already downloaded may remain there and remain playable; what a refund removes is the unlock on our side.
- **(d) Not a penalty.** Revocation after a refund is not a penalty; it returns you and Audioura LLC to the position before the purchase.

## 4. What is not a refund
- **(a) Failed or interrupted downloads.** A download that fails or is interrupted does not use up your purchase. You may retry at no extra charge until it completes.
- **(b) App updates.** An app update installs in place and does not remove tours you already downloaded.
- **(c) Recovery code — a lost or replaced phone.** If you lose or replace your phone, you can restore your **Listen page** (the paid tours, free tours and news you had) on the new phone with your **recovery code**, at no extra charge. Restoring your Listen page this way is not a new purchase and does not affect your right to ask Apple or Google for a refund.

## 5. Community passes
A pass gives access for a fixed period — a day, a week, a month or a year, as set by that community — and does not auto-renew. If Apple or Google refunds a pass, your membership ends at the next online check and you stop receiving new audio from that community. Audio you downloaded while the pass was active may remain on your device, but you will not receive further updates.

## 6. Languages
Each language of a tour is a **separate product** with its own price, listed separately before purchase. A refund for one language does not affect other languages you purchased.

## 7. Non-transferable
Purchases, passes, recovery codes and Listen pages are non-transferable and may not be resold.
