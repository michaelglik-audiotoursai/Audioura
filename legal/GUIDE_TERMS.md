These Guide Terms apply when you price and sell an audio tour on Audioura. They are in addition to the general [Terms of Service](/terms). By pricing a tour, you accept these Guide Terms.

## 1. Pricing
You set a price for your tour from Audioura's **price ladder** (a fixed set of allowed prices). You pick a price point; you do not enter an arbitrary amount. Listeners buy your tour **per language** — each translation is sold separately. Each purchase pays for **one download** of your tour: a listener who buys can download it once to their device and then play it offline forever, and buys again if they delete it or move to a new phone.

## 2. Evaluation and content rating
Every tour on Audioura is evaluated automatically before and while it is listed:
- an **Audioura score** (a quality number produced by a model that reads your tour's text), shown to listeners the same way for every tour; and
- a **content rating** — General, Mature (18+), or Not allowed — decided by a model, not a word list.

These apply to your tour too. A Mature tour is shown only to listeners who confirmed they are 18+. A Not-allowed tour is never listed. See the [Content Policy](/content-policy).

## 3. Review queue
A tour that is not clearly **General** waits in a **review queue** and is not listed publicly until it has been reviewed. This protects listeners and keeps Audioura compliant with Apple/Google rules.

## 4. Your responsibility for your content
You are responsible for the accuracy, legality, and originality of your tour and of **any edits you make** to it. You confirm you have the right to publish the content and that it follows the Content Policy and the law. If you edit a tour, the edited version is yours — Audioura's automated score may re-run, and you may request one free re-score after an edit.

## 5. Licence to Audioura
You keep ownership of your tour. You grant Audioura a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free licence to **store, list, display, sell, and deliver** your tour to listeners through the app, including the free Stop 1 preview and each paid download a listener buys.

## 6. Payouts
**Payouts to guides are not built yet.** The exact payout terms — share, schedule, minimums, and the method — will be **announced before the first payout**. Until payouts exist, pricing a tour sets its listener price and exercises the evaluation and listing flow; it does not create a payment obligation from Audioura to you for sales that occur before payout terms are published and accepted. We will not pay out under terms you have not had the chance to see and accept.

## 7. Removal and termination
Audioura may remove a tour that breaks these terms, the Content Policy, or the law, or that fails review. You may stop offering a tour at any time; listeners who already bought it keep what they bought.
