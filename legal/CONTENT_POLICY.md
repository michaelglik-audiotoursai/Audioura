## In short
Every Audioura tour gets a rating, like a movie rating. The rating is assigned automatically by a model that reads the tour's **meaning in context**, not by matching keywords — keyword lists wrongly flag ordinary words like "class", "Assisi" or "Scunthorpe". Ratings are best-effort and can be wrong in either direction. If one looks wrong, report it.

- **General** — for everyone. History, art, architecture, nature, culture, and food and drink in ordinary context.
- **Mature (18+)** — shown only if you turn on "Show 18+ tours" and confirm you are 18 or older. May include strong language, or tours focused on alcohol, nightlife, gambling, or the history of crime.
- **Not listed** — not shown on Audioura. Explicit sexual content, adult services, hate speech, or instructions for illegal activity.

Community audio is hosted by the organiser and is not rated by Audioura.

## The detail

### How a rating is assigned
A model reads the tour and assigns one of the three ratings below, with a short reason. It judges meaning in context rather than individual words.

Ratings are automated and best-effort. They are not a guarantee that a tour is suitable for any particular listener, and the model can be wrong in either direction — it can rate a tour higher than it needed to be, or miss something. Where the model's answer is unclear, the more restrictive rating applies.

A tour is rated before it is listed. A new or edited tour that cannot be rated yet is held, not listed, until its rating is in place.

### General — available to everyone
Factual treatment of history, art, architecture, nature, culture, and food and drink in ordinary context. For example: a history or architecture walk; a museum tour; a restaurant tour that happens to include a place that serves drinks; a nature or park tour.

Activities such as biking or dog-sledding are rated on their content, not on physical safety; safety is covered by our [Terms](/terms).

General tours are ordinarily listed immediately.

### Mature (18+) — available only to listeners who have confirmed they are 18 or older
For example: strong or pervasive profanity; a tour whose primary purpose is drinking, such as a bar crawl, wine or spirits tasting, or cocktail bars; **tours focused on the history of crime**, gambling and casinos, or nightlife as entertainment.

A Mature tour is held for review and, once approved, is listed only to listeners who have turned on "Show 18+ tours" in Settings. That setting is off by default, and turning it on is the listener's own statement that they are 18 or older — Audioura does not verify age.

### Not listed
For example: sexual services or adult entertainment; explicit sexual content; hate speech or content that demeans people on the basis of a protected characteristic; content that **encourages, enables or instructs unlawful activity** (for example, a tour explaining how to commit a crime, as distinct from the history of one).

A tour rated this way is held and is not listed on Audioura. We also reserve the right to remove content and to suspend or close an account under our Terms.

### Review, and when a rating changes
Anything not clearly General is held for review before it is listed, and the more restrictive rating applies while it waits. We do not promise a review within any particular time, and nothing here is a promise that any specific tour has been reviewed and approved.

A rating may change after review, or if a tour is edited and re-rated.

A translation inherits the rating of the original tour. Translating a tour never lowers its rating: if the original is Mature, every translation of it is Mature.

### Reports
If you believe a rating is wrong, or that a tour breaks these rules, use **Report** in the app — it opens an email to info@audioura.com. We may correct a rating, or stop listing a tour, after review. Reports about community audio are routed to the community owner.

### Community (organiser-hosted) audio
Everything above applies to tours Audioura generates. Community audio is hosted by the organiser. Audioura stores only metadata such as titles and links, and does not inspect or rate organiser-hosted audio. Organisers are solely responsible for their community audio under the [Organiser Terms](/organiser-terms). Audioura retains the ability to disable a community that breaks these rules.
