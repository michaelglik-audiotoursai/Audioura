Every tour on Audioura gets a content rating, like a movie rating. The rating is decided by an AI model that reads the tour's **meaning in context** — not a banned-word list (word lists wrongly flag ordinary words like "class", "Assisi", or "Scunthorpe"). Tours that are not clearly **General** wait for human review before they are listed. This policy is part of the [Terms of Service](/terms).

## The three ratings

### General — listed for everyone
Suitable for a general audience. Factual history, art, architecture, nature, culture, food and drink in ordinary context.

**Examples (General):**
- A history or architecture walking tour.
- A museum or sculpture-park tour.
- A **restaurant tour** — including **a restaurant that serves drinks**. A restaurant serving alcohol with a meal is **General**.
- A nature or park tour (including physical-activity tours like biking or dog-sledding, which are rated on content, with safety covered by the Terms).

### Mature (18+) — listed only to confirmed-18+ listeners
Content aimed at adults or focused on adult themes. Shown only to listeners who have confirmed they are 18 or older (the app's "Show 18+ tours" setting).

**Examples (Mature):**
- Strong or pervasive profanity.
- A tour **focused on alcohol** — for example a bar crawl, a wine- or spirits-tasting tour, or a venue whose main point is drinking (e.g. a cocktail bar).
- Crime, gambling/casinos, or nightlife as the tour's focus.

### Not allowed — never listed
Content Audioura will not host.

**Examples (Not allowed):**
- Sexual services or adult entertainment; explicit sexual content.
- Hate speech or content that demeans people based on who they are.
- Content that encourages or instructs illegal acts.

## How it is applied
- **General** tours list immediately.
- **Mature** tours are held for review, then listed only to confirmed-18+ listeners.
- **Not allowed** tours are held for review and are not listed.
- Anything the model is unsure about defaults to **the safer outcome** and waits for human review.
- **Translations inherit the original tour's rating.**
- A rating can be reviewed by a person in Audioura's review queue, which can approve or reject a held tour.

## Community (owner-hosted) audio
Audioura's automated content rating applies to **Audioura-generated tours**. Community audio is **hosted by the organiser**, and Audioura stores only its metadata — so Audioura does **not** inspect owner-hosted audio. Organisers are responsible for their audio following this policy and the law; see the [Organiser Terms](/organiser-terms). Reports about a community route to its owner, and Audioura keeps a per-community off switch.

## Reporting
If you think a tour or community breaks this policy, use the report option in the app. Community reports go to the community owner; Audioura can disable a community that breaks the rules.
