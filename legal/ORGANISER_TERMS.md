These Organiser Terms apply when you create and run a community on Audioura. They are in addition to the general [Terms of Service](/terms). By creating a community, you accept these Organiser Terms.

## 1. You are the publisher of record
A community is **yours**. You are the **publisher of record** for the community and its audio. You decide the name, description, your organiser details, the rules, who may join, and what audio is included.

## 2. You own and host your audio
You **host your own audio** (as a list of MP3 links or a single podcast/RSS feed). **Audioura stores only metadata — titles, order, and the links/feed URL — not your audio files.** Because Audioura does not store your files, **Audioura does not review or check your audio**. You are responsible for ensuring your audio is lawful, that you have the rights to it, and that it follows the law and the [Content Policy](/content-policy). (Audioura's automated content rating applies to Audioura-generated tours; it does not inspect owner-hosted community audio.)

## 3. Member waivers are between you and your members
If your community uses a **waiver** (for example for a physical activity), that waiver is an agreement **between you and your members** — Audioura is not a party to it. A community that uses a waiver is always **Private** (not listed publicly).

## 4. Listed vs Private
- **Listed:** your community can appear in search and on the map; it must have a location.
- **Private:** your community is reachable only by its invite code; it does not appear on the map. Waiver communities are always Private.

## 5. Members keep what they downloaded
Members join with your invite code and (for a paid community) buy a **pass** that lasts a **fixed length of time** and **does not renew automatically**. When a member's pass ends, they stop getting **new** audio, but any audio they already **downloaded** stays on their device. You can report problems and Audioura keeps a per-community **off switch**; a report about a community tour is routed to **you**, the owner.

## 6. Payments
Passes are sold through the **Apple App Store** or **Google Play**. **Apple or Google is the seller to the member**: they take the payment,
handle card details, refunds and sales tax/VAT, and pay the proceeds to Audioura after their commission. Neither you nor Audioura sees card details.
Passes do not renew automatically; a member buys again to continue. Your share of the proceeds is paid by Audioura under the payout terms (Section 7).

## 7. Payouts
**Payouts to organisers are not built yet.** The payout terms — share, schedule, and method — will be **announced before the first payout.** Until then, creating a community and offering passes exercises the join, pass, and content flow; it does not create a payment obligation from Audioura to you for sales that occur before payout terms are published and accepted.

## 8. Removal and the off switch
Audioura may disable a community that breaks these terms, the Content Policy, or the law, or in response to a valid report or legal demand. You may stop running a community at any time; members keep audio they already downloaded.
