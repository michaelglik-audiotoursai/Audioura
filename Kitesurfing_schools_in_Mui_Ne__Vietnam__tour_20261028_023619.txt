Stop 1: Vietnam Kiteboarding School

Coordinates: 10.9512, 108.2499

Orientation: You are about to embark on a walking journey through kitesurfing schools in Mui Ne, Vietnam. Your first stop is Vietnam Kiteboarding School. The vibrant colors of the kites contrast sharply with the azure sky, setting the scene for an exhilarating experience.

Founded in 2007 by Mr. Hai, the Vietnam Kiteboarding School stands as a beacon of innovation in Mui Mr. Hai was one of the first local instructors to receive certification from the International Kiteboarding Organization (IKO), marking a pivotal moment for Vietnamese water sports. His dedication to kiteboarding not only elevated the sport locally but also attracted enthusiasts from around the globe who sought expert guidance in the art of harnessing the wind. The school has been the backdrop for dramatic stories, such as the harrowing incident in January 2025. An experienced Russian kitesurfer went missing after being caught in treacherous weather conditions off Ham Tien beach. As the sun set and the wind picked up, he drifted five nautical miles into open waters. His ordeal lasted nearly eight hours, a grueling fight against the elements. At 1:30 AM, local fishermen, skilled in the ways of the sea, found him 1.5 miles off Ong Dia beach and brought him safely back. This rescue reflected the enduring community spirit and sea expertise of Mui Ne's local fishing community. In early 2026, a separate event unfolded on Nguyen Dinh Chieu road, showcasing the sometimes volatile intersection of local and international cultures. A dispute between a foreign national and a local taxi driver escalated, tragically resulting in the stabbing of a police officer who had responded to the scene. The incident sparked a dramatic 70-kilometer motorized police pursuit, putting the town in the international spotlight and highlighting the challenges that can arise in a bustling tourist destination. Today, the Vietnam Kiteboarding School not only teaches the sport but also serves as a symbol of resilience and community. As the winds guide you further along the coast, the stories of courage and collaboration echo in the air, inviting you to explore the next chapter of this vibrant coastal community.

Directions: From Vietnam Kiteboarding School, head towards the beach and stroll along Nguyen Dinh Chieu Street. Keep the ocean on your right as you walk, and soon you’ll spot Surfpoint Kiteboarding School nestled among the palm trees. It’s just a short walk down the street, so enjoy the sea breeze as you make your way there!

Stop 2: Surfpoint Kiteboarding School

Address:

Coordinates: 10.9433, 108.2491

Orientation: Look towards the horizon, where Surfpoint Kiteboarding School stands as a testament to the evolution of sport and culture in this vibrant coastal town.

Surfpoint Kiteboarding School was co-founded in 2009 by Phong, a local who began his journey as a beach boy in 2003, and Peter, a European physical education instructor. Phong’s rise from self-taught rider to celebrated instructor is a story of determination and triumph. By 2006 and 2007, he had swept the 'King of Mui Ne' competitions, cementing his status in the kitesurfing community. The main beach strip of Mui Ne is more than just a playground for kitesurfers; it is a cultural melting pot. Schools here cater to diverse linguistic groups, with some focusing exclusively on Russian-speaking tourists. This mix of languages and cultures creates lively scenes during the peak windy seasons. Instructors often recount tales of shouting matches and tangled kite lines, illustrating the challenges of communication in this multicultural hub. Beyond the thrill of the kites and the stories of rivalry and camaraderie, the beach holds deeper layers of history. The nearby Cham ruins stand as silent witnesses to centuries past, reminding visitors of the historical depth beneath the sands.

Directions: Keep walking until you spot the distinctive C2Sky Kite Center sign on your left, just past a few local eateries. It’s an easy walk, so enjoy the ocean breeze as you make your way there! It is roughly 200 meters away.

Stop 3: C2Sky Kite Center

Coordinates: 10.9434, 108.2474

Orientation: As you stand on the wind-beaten sands of Mui Ne, envision over a hundred kites dancing in the sky each December to February. Here at the C2Sky Kite Center, distinguished by its strictly certified International Kiteboarding Organization (IKO) status, there's more at stake in the swift tailwinds than leisure.

Unlike the Surfpoint Kiteboarding School you visited earlier, this center focuses on safety. During peak season, the skies fill with kites weaving in and out of each other's flight paths, sometimes colliding in mid-air, creating a dramatic spectacle for those grounded below. The degradation — a relentless assault by the sea — creates a precarious edge for kite launching during high tide. Here, caution mingles with thrill as adventurers test skill against nature's ever-shifting boundaries. The center is more than a collection of kites and aerial mechanics. It presents a diversion with its blend of kitesurfing and yoga retreats. The dance you supported in Surfpoint now finds a moments' mediation, as practitioners unite movement, breath, and balance, reinforcing the cyclic nature of strength and serenity that the sea demands. As you ascend the nearby dune, the horizon opens into an ever-expanding canvas, where the lineage of the Cham people melds with today's kitesurfers—a continuum of mastery and storytelling carried by the wind.

This tour highlights the vibrant interplay of community, culture, and adventure that defines the kitesurfing scene in Mui Ne. As you reflect on the stories of resilience and collaboration, remember that the winds of this coastal paradise carry not just kites, but the spirit of its people and their shared experiences.
