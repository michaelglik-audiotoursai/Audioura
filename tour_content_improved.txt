Stop 1: Gallery NAGA

Orientation: You can find Gallery NAGA at 67 Newbury St, Boston, MA 02116. For GPS navigation, use coordinates: 42.3500, -71.0762.

Gallery NAGA is a contemporary art gallery showcasing emerging and established artists. It is open Tuesday-Saturday 10:00 AM - 5:30 PM, Sunday 12:00 PM - 5:00 PM, closed Mondays. Free admission, artworks range from $500 to $15,000. No reservations needed but group tours available by appointment. It is known for their vibrant abstract paintings by local Boston artists, innovative mixed media installations featuring recycled materials, and thought-provoking sculptures that explore urban themes. Allow extra time to fully appreciate the exhibits and consider guided tour options in the gallery or with me, your personalized guiding companion.

Please resume the tour at our next stop by following these directions: From Gallery NAGA, head east on Newbury Street for 2 blocks, then turn left onto Massachusetts Avenue. Walk north for 4 blocks, then turn right onto Commonwealth Avenue. Panopticon Gallery is located on the right inside Hotel Commonwealth at the corner of Charlesgate East.

Stop 2: Panopticon Gallery

Orientation: You can find Panopticon Gallery at 502c Commonwealth Ave, Boston, MA 02215. For GPS navigation, use coordinates: 42.3495, -71.0955.

Panopticon Gallery is a photography gallery featuring contemporary and vintage photographic works. It is open Wednesday-Saturday 11:00 AM - 6:00 PM, Sunday 1:00 PM - 5:00 PM. Free admission, prints available for purchase starting at $200. Located inside Hotel Commonwealth, accessible through main lobby. It showcases black and white street photography capturing Boston's urban life, iconic portraits of jazz musicians from the 1960s-80s, and stunning New England landscape photographs including Cape Cod seascapes. Allow extra time to fully appreciate the exhibits and consider guided tour options in the gallery or with me, your personalized guiding companion.

Please resume the tour at our next stop by following these directions: From Panopticon Gallery, head south on Massachusetts Avenue for 6 blocks to Boylston Street. Turn right on Boylston and walk west for 3 blocks to Tremont Street. Turn left on Tremont, then right on Herald Street, and left on Harrison Avenue. Galatea Fine Art is in the SoWa Art + Design District on the left side, entrance through the courtyard.

Stop 3: Galatea Fine Art

Orientation: You can find Galatea Fine Art at 460 Harrison Ave, #B6, Boston, MA 02118. For GPS navigation, use coordinates: 42.3420, -71.0665.

Galatea Fine Art is an artist-run contemporary art gallery featuring local and regional talents. It is open Wednesday-Sunday 12:00 PM - 6:00 PM, closed Monday-Tuesday. Free admission, artworks range from $100 to $8,000. First Friday openings with artist talks at 7:00 PM. It features colorful abstract paintings inspired by Boston Harbor, intricate mixed media collages incorporating vintage Boston Globe clippings, and experimental sculptures made from reclaimed materials from local construction sites. Allow extra time to fully appreciate the exhibits and consider guided tour options in the gallery or with me, your personalized guiding companion.
