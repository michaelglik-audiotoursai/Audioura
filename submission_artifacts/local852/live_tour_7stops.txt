Stop 1: Rambutan

Coordinates: 10.7761, 106.6950

Type/Specialty: Restaurant and Bar

Specific Examples: Tropical cocktails, live music, vibrant atmosphere

Orientation: You are about to embark on a walking journey through Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam. This backdrop serves as a reminder of the passage of time and the resilience of the people, inviting you to discover the layers of identity that thrive in today’s Ho Chi Minh City. Your first stop is Rambutan. From Ben Thanh Market, head south on Lê Lợi Street, then turn right onto Nguyễn Thái Bình Street. Rambutan will be on your left. Look for a cluster of old trees with their distinctive spiky green-and-red fruit hanging low, shading the pavement and casting dappled light on the worn sidewalks nearby.

The thick canopy of rambutan trees here offers more than shade—it holds a quiet trace of this neighborhood’s connection to the region’s agricultural rhythms. While the exact age of these trees is uncertain, their presence evokes the long tradition of tropical fruit cultivation in southern Vietnam. The rambutan’s leathery skin, covered in soft spines, cracks open to reveal a juicy, translucent flesh that locals have enjoyed for generations. The street hums faintly with the sounds of daily life: motorbikes weaving past, street vendors calling out their wares, and the occasional rustle of leaves stirred by the breeze.

Nearby, the distant outline of Long Thanh International Airport’s construction site marks a vast shift in this region’s future. Approved by the Vietnamese government on January 4, 2021, with work beginning the day after, the airport is planned as a second gateway for Ho Chi Minh City, aiming to relieve the pressured Tan Son Nhat Airport. Tan Son Nhat serves as the busiest airport in Vietnam but cannot grow further due to being hemmed in by dense urban buildings. Long Thanh’s development, expected to finish by late 2026, represents the city’s expanding reach beyond its current borders. The fruit’s seasonal cycle mirrors the blend of continuity and transformation defining this part of Ho Chi Minh City.

Directions: As you stroll along, keep an eye out for the beautiful architecture of the nearby buildings. Continue until you reach the intersection with Le Van Sy Street, where you'll find Longan just a short walk ahead on your right. Enjoy the vibrant atmosphere as you make your way!

Stop 2: Longan

Coordinates: 10.7765, 106.6938

Type/Specialty: Bar

Specific Examples: Signature cocktails, local beers, laid-back vibe

The hum of nearby motorbikes blends with the chatter of sellers, while the faint scent of ripening fruit rises in the warm air.

This street, named Longan, carries echoes of a tale passed down along the Mekong’s winding waters. Local tradition holds that a poor boy once discovered a dragon’s egg by the riverbank. When the egg hatched, the creature that emerged was not a fire-breathing terror, but a small dragon whose breath sprayed water, nurturing the village’s parched fields during harsh dry seasons. This dragon became a guardian, its watery spray a vital force sustaining life here.

The story goes that the dragon’s right eye was stolen by an evil raven, who gave it to a robber. The boy and the dragon confronted this thief in a fierce struggle to recover the eye. Though they triumphed in driving the robber away, the eye was destroyed in the fight, leaving the dragon blind.

This place resonates with the broader rhythms of the Mekong Delta, where Cần Thơ, the third largest centrally-governed city in Vietnam with a population nearing 4.2 million as of 2024, serves as a vital hub. On June 12, 2025, the city expanded when the National Assembly passed Resolution No. 202/2025/QH15, merging neighboring Hậu Giang and Sóc Trăng provinces into the municipality. The longan’s quiet symbolism parallels this unfolding of the city’s boundaries and identity—a fruit both rooted in tradition and carried forward by change.

Directions: Start by walking along Nguyen Thi Minh Khai Street, where you can take in the local shops and cafes. As you stroll, keep an eye out for the bright colors of the street art that line the walls. When you reach the intersection with Le Duan Street, continue straight, and soon you'll find yourself on The Crazy Street, buzzing with energy and excitement. Enjoy the vibrant atmosphere! It is about a minute's walk away.

Stop 3: The Crazy street

Coordinates: 10.7759, 106.6935

Type/Specialty: Street

Specific Examples: Vibrant nightlife, street food vendors, bustling crowds

Orientation: Stand near the entrance to The Crazy street, where narrow lanes twist between neon signs and the hum of motorbikes fills the air. The chaotic energy here contrasts with the calm you found near the Longan stop, shifting the pace and texture of the city.

The Crazy street earns its name from the swirl of activity and layered history that plays out in its tight corners and tangled wires overhead. Like the intersecting streets in Gustave Caillebotte’s 1877 painting “Paris Street; Rainy Day,” which you saw at the Art Institute of Chicago, The Crazy street presents a shifting tableau where old patterns meet modern impulse. The textures here—the rough concrete walls, flickering signage, and pungent spices—anchor the street in the present moment.

Though The Crazy street itself does not directly connect to the 1975 spring offensive, that final North Vietnamese campaign reshaped the city’s fate. In December 1974, the People’s Army of Vietnam crossed from Cambodia and captured Phước Long Province by January 1975. This victory expanded their offensive, leading to the capture of Buôn Ma Thuột on 18 March. These military advances pushed South Vietnamese forces into strategic withdrawals, setting the stage for the city you now walk through.

Directions: From The Crazy street, head east on Bùi Viện Street, then turn right onto Nguyễn Thái Bình Street. Thi Bar Saigon will be on your left. You might notice colorful murals as you walk by. Continue to The LGBT bar 🌈Thi Bar Saigon — it is roughly 900 meters away.

Stop 4: The LGBT bar 🌈Thi Bar Saigon

Coordinates: 10.767660, 106.693960

Type/Specialty: LGBT Bar

Specific Examples: Drag shows, themed nights, inclusive atmosphere

Orientation: Stand just outside the modest entrance of Thi Bar Saigon, its neon rainbow sign a vivid contrast to the surrounding street’s more conventional facades.

Thi Bar Saigon emerged in 2016 as one of the first LGBT-friendly venues in Ho Chi Minh City, a city still navigating the evolving landscape of LGBT rights. Its significance lies in offering a safe space where people could assemble and express their identities openly—a quiet but meaningful defiance in a traditionally conservative society. The bar’s modest interior, with vibrant colors and a welcoming layout, stands in sharp contrast to the surrounding streets, including The Crazy street you passed earlier, which bustle with their own energy but lack this explicit embrace of diversity.

In 2018, Thi Bar Saigon became a pivotal venue during the annual VietPride celebrations, an event dedicated to raising awareness and visibility for the LGBT community in Vietnam. That year, the bar hosted a significant gathering where voices that were often marginalized found amplification. This event brought together local activists and allies, attracting attention from broader circles and helping to shift public conversations around inclusion. The bar’s role in this celebration anchored it firmly in the city’s social fabric, transforming it from a simple nightlife spot into a landmark of cultural progress.

Inside, the steady rhythm of music, the soft glow of colored lights, and the mingling voices create a sensory layer that connects the bar’s present to a wider story of community and change.

Directions: From Thi Bar Saigon, head down Tran Khanh Du street towards the bustling area. As you stroll, keep an eye out for the vibrant street art and local shops along the way. When you reach the intersection with Nguyen Thi Minh Khai, continue straight, and you’ll find A Star is Born just a bit further down on your left. Enjoy the lively atmosphere as you make your way there! It is roughly 800 meters away.

Stop 5: A Star is Born

Coordinates: 10.7746, 106.6962

Type/Specialty: Karaoke Bar

Specific Examples: Private karaoke rooms, extensive song list, lively crowd

Orientation: Look for the simple doorway that opens onto a space where creativity quietly gathers.

Hồ Chí Minh, born Nguyễn Sinh Cung in 1890 near Vinh, is a figure whose many pseudonyms reflect the multiplicity of roles one might assume—an idea that resonates here. This place has been linked to emerging artistic voices, though the precise names and works remain elusive. Its presence amid Ho Chi Minh City’s evolving cultural fabric suggests a hidden network where modern expression took root. The air carries the murmur of collaboration and reinvention, yet the full story behind A Star is Born remains a matter of local lore and quiet speculation.

Directions: Keep an eye out for the bustling shops and cafes as you stroll along. When you reach the corner with Ngô Thời Nhiệm Street, turn left, and you'll find Acoustic Bar just a short walk ahead on your right. Continue to Acoustic Bar - Ngô Thời Nhiệm, Quận 3.

Stop 6: Acoustic Bar - Ngô Thời Nhiệm, Quận 3

Coordinates: 10.781195, 106.689920

Type/Specialty: Live Music Bar

Specific Examples: Local bands, cozy atmosphere, open mic nights

Orientation: The Acoustic Bar sits on Ngô Thời Nhiệm Street, tucked behind a modest facade that hums quietly compared to the bustle of Quận 3 around it. The narrow street here offers a brief moment of calm, a prelude to the music that draws people inside.

Acoustic Bar became a key venue for live local music starting in the early 2000s, anchoring its significance in Ho Chi Minh City’s evolving cultural scene. Before the crowds that gather here now, local musicians found a platform in this small space, where the sound of acoustic guitars and vocals was not drowned out by the city's noise but amplified as a shared language. The bar’s role in fostering this vibrant music scene is not just about providing a place to play; it’s about creating a community where artists and listeners intersect. The faint scent of brewed coffee and occasional hints of incense mingle with the raw timber of the bar’s interior, a tactile reminder of the intimate setting where this music unfolds.

One story that stands out happened in 2015, when the Vietnamese rock band Bức Tường performed here. This was one of their last performances before their lead singer, Trần Lập, passed away in 2016. Their presence carried a weight that night, marking a significant moment in the band’s history and in the bar’s legacy as a stage for important musical milestones. By becoming a hotspot for these artists, the bar helped carve out a niche where original Vietnamese songwriting could flourish in a raw, unamplified form. The hum of strings here echoes the same spirit found in A Star is Born, where music breathes life into personal and collective stories.

Directions: As you stroll, keep an eye out for the lively cafes and shops lining the street. After a short walk, you'll reach the corner of Trần Quốc Thảo Street, where you can find An Spa just a bit further down on your left.

Stop 7: An Spa

Coordinates: 10.7740, 106.6945

Type/Specialty: Spa

Specific Examples: Traditional Vietnamese massages, tranquil environment, wellness treatments

Orientation: An Spa is discreetly tucked amid the hum of District 3’s streets, not far from the corner of Đồng Khởi Street and Lam Sơn Square. The spa’s unassuming entrance reveals a quieter realm, a contrast to the lively sidewalks surrounding it.

The spa’s presence near landmarks like the Hôtel Continental Saïgon, which first opened in 1880 during French Indochina, places it within a neighborhood shaped by layers of history and commerce. The Continental, recognized as the oldest hotel in Vietnam, anchors this corner where Đồng Khởi Street—once Rue N°6—served as a busy commercial artery from the Saigon River bank. The story goes that this spa carries on a local tradition of herbal remedies and bodywork passed through generations, mirroring the city’s ongoing negotiation between tradition and modern life. The subtle fragrance inside mingles with distant street sounds, creating a sensory link between the private calm here and the surrounding urban energy already glimpsed in stops like the rambutan stall. This connection between inner refreshment and the city’s movement traces the living pulse of Ho Chi Minh City itself.

Together, these stops reveal the intricate interplay between tradition and transformation in Ho Chi Minh City, highlighting how local stories and cultural expressions shape the urban landscape. This dynamic connection invites us to appreciate the rich tapestry of identity and community that continues to evolve in this vibrant city. That's 7 stops in all.
