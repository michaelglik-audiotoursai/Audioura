Around the corner, passing motorbikes and a steady city breeze carry scents of grilled street food and fresh herbs. Rambutan offers a quiet corner amid the city’s bustle, shaped by the steady flow of travelers and locals. The space breathes a lived-in comfort, with faded wooden tables, soft lighting, and walls that display postcards or sketches pinned up by visitors from many places. The smell of hops and malt mingles with hints of tropical fruits, anchoring Rambutan to its locale. As a hostel-bar hybrid, Rambutan reflects Ho Chi Minh City’s rise as a hub where old trade routes and new currents of tourism cross paths. Though the details of its founding are not firmly recorded, it fits into a broader pattern of spaces responding to the city’s relentless growth and changing rhythms. Sounds of laughter and conversation echo stories of transition—both from those arriving from afar and those rooted in the city’s everyday life. Stop 2: Longan

Coordinates: 10.7755, 106.6959

Type/Specialty: Restaurant

Specific Examples: Delicious Vietnamese cuisine, fresh longan fruit dishes, cozy dining area.

Orientation: Position yourself to best view this location.

Longan restaurant, in this part of Ho Chi Minh City, offers more than just a taste of Vietnamese cuisine; it stands as a quiet testament to the region’s connection to stories woven through time and symbolized in the very fruit it serves. This egg hatched a dragon that helped the village survive harsh dry seasons by spraying water upon the fields. Yet the story’s shadow crept in with a sinister turn. The raven did not keep the eye but handed it over to a robber, who sought to use the dragon’s power for his own ambitions. The young boy, bound by loyalty and courage to protect his village and the dragon, confronted the robber. Armed with only his bow and arrow, the boy defeated the robber, reclaiming the stolen eye. Tragically, the dragon’s eye was destroyed when it fell onto the sand, a loss captured in the name itself—longan, or dragon’s eye—preserving the memory of this sacrifice. Within Longan’s humble walls, the sound of clinking chopsticks blends with the soft murmur of conversation, underscoring the continuity of centuries-old stories. The simple textures of paper lanterns and wood paneling around you carry a quiet warmth, holding those legends that breathe beneath the surface of every shared meal. Here, the story of a boy, a dragon, and a stolen eye lingers—not as a mere tale but in the taste and scent that flow through the air, promising sustenance and remembrance alike.

Directions: Continue on Đường Nguyễn Thái Bình for about 5 minutes, then turn right onto Bùi Viện; The Crazy street will be ahead. The sounds of laughter and music will fill the air as you approach.

Stop 4: The LGBT bar 🌈Thi Bar Saigon

Coordinates: 10.767660, 106.693960

Type/Specialty: LGBT Bar

Specific Examples: Inclusive atmosphere, themed nights, vibrant cocktails.

Orientation: This stop unfolds along a lively stretch you've already glimpsed near The Crazy Street. A vibrant venue where neon lights splash colors across the sidewalk and music pulses softly through the open door can be found here. The air carries a mix of chatter, laughter, and the distant clink of glasses — a sensory invitation to a gathering place where acceptance sets the tone.

Thi Bar Saigon has undergone a transformation within its walls. Once just another neighborhood venue, it now stands as a gathering place for Ho Chi Minh City’s LGBT community. Before one pivotal night, drag performances in the city were rare and mostly hidden from public view. That evening changed the pattern—by opening its stage to drag artists, the bar helped increase visibility and acceptance for LGBT lives in a place where silence often prevailed. It became a focal point where activists and community members met to strategize around LGBT rights. The conversations here influenced how the community planned public expressions of identity, negotiated rights, and sought visibility in Vietnam’s social landscape.

Inside, the bar carries the lively echoes of those years. The clinking of glasses mixes with layered beats rolling from the polished speakers overhead. Painted in shades of midnight blue with splashes of neon, the space reflects the party’s inclusivity. The scent of spiced cocktails and fresh limes blends with laughter and shouts of camaraderie—a sensory echo of the community’s resilience. This stop connects directly to the tour’s earlier focus on The Crazy street, once a place of nocturnal freedom and defiance.

Directions: Start by cycling out of Thi Bar Saigon onto Bùi Viện Street, where you can soak in the vibrant atmosphere of the area. Pedal towards Trần Quốc Thảo Street, enjoying the local sights along the way. Once you reach Trần Quốc Thảo, ride along until you spot A Star is Born on your left. Enjoy the ride! It is roughly 1.6 km (about a 20-minute walk) away.

Stop 5: A Star is Born

Coordinates: 10.7824, 106.6942

Type/Specialty: Karaoke Bar

Specific Examples: Private karaoke rooms, extensive song list, lively crowd.

Orientation: Look for a narrow entrance painted bright red along this bustling street.

The private rooms provide a contrast to the crowded streets outside, wrapping each group in soundproofed intimacy. Many of the songs available here reflect the shifts in Vietnamese culture over time. While there is no direct tie to Hồ Chí Minh, born Nguyễn Sinh Cung, the acts of singing and gathering recall the countless renditions of patriotic and folk songs that once fueled public sentiment across the city. The faint buzz of neon hues sets the bar apart from the older streets you passed, yet it remains firmly part of the city’s ongoing narrative—a chapter of song emerging from a complex history.

Directions: When you reach the intersection, turn onto Ngô Thời Nhiệm and continue riding until you spot the Acoustic Bar on your left, just after passing by some charming shops. It’s a nice, relaxed route that gives you a taste of the area! Continue to Acoustic Bar - Ngô Thời Nhiệm, Quận 3 — it is roughly 500 meters away.

Stop 6: Acoustic Bar - Ngô Thời Nhiệm, Quận 3

Coordinates: 10.781195, 106.689920

Type/Specialty: Live Music Bar

Specific Examples: Live performances, local artists, intimate setting.

Orientation: Park your bike just off Ngô Thời Nhiệm, where the narrow alley splits subtly between two low, dark facades. The flicker of warm yellow light through sliding glass windows is your signal: you’ve found the Acoustic Bar.

Acoustic Bar is a place where fresh voices find eager ears and the city's musical life pulses steadily forward. The room itself seems to catch the vibrations from the stage—a patchwork of wood, softly reflecting the stage lights, with the faint scent of brewing coffee hanging in the air. Acoustic Bar hosts weekly sessions where emerging artists test new material in front of an intimate crowd. These live shows attract a blend of musicians and fans, creating a lively atmosphere where local artistry grows organically within this neighborhood setting. As a result, artists who might otherwise struggle to connect directly with audiences find a regular spotlight here, making the bar a humble yet vital thread in the city's cultural fabric.

One story from Acoustic Bar’s recent past involves a local songwriter who credits her early gigs here with securing her first record deal. After a string of performances, her blend of personal lyrics and acoustic melodies caught the attention of a producer in attendance, leading to a recording contract that launched her career beyond the city’s bustling streets. This confirms the venue’s role as a crucial stepping stone for musical hopes.

Its intimate live music and shared conversations leave a lingering impression—something softer but no less alive.

Directions: Start by cycling out of Acoustic Bar and head towards Ngô Thời Nhiệm Street. Pedal your way to Trần Quốc Thảo Street, where you'll find a lovely tree-lined route. Continue biking along Trần Quốc Thảo and keep an eye out for the charming local shops and cafes as you approach An Spa. It is roughly 400 meters away.
