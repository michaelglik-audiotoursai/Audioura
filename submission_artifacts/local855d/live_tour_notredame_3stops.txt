Stop 1: Notre-Dame Cathedral Basilica of Saigon
Address: 1 Công xã Paris, Bến Nghé, Quận 1, Hồ Chí Minh, Vietnam
Coordinates: 10.779722, 106.699167
Type/Specialty: Historical Landmark
Specific Examples: Stunning neo-Romanesque architecture, beautiful stained glass windows, and a serene garden area.
Orientation: As you traverse this route, you will uncover how the echoes of wartime struggles and architectural legacies intertwine, revealing the resilience of a city that continues to evolve. Your first stop is Notre-Dame Cathedral Basilica of Saigon. The cathedral will be on your right. You might notice the bustling atmosphere of the surrounding cafés. As you step onto the broad plaza in front of the cathedral, the sharp clang of the two towering bells rings out from the 58-meter-high bell towers, mingling with the chatter of visitors and the distant hum of the city, drawing attention upward to the striking red brick facade. The Notre-Dame Cathedral Basilica of Saigon rises with a commanding presence, its structure crafted between 1877 and 1880 under the direction of French administrators who first named it the Church of Saigon. Every red brick that composes its walls was imported from Marseille, France, marking the cathedral’s direct link to its colonial origins. The twin bell towers, each soaring 58 meters above the ground, punctuate the skyline and hold bells that have called generations to prayer and community gatherings. A significant chapter unfolded in 1959 when a statue of the Virgin Mary, known as Our Lady of Peace, was installed in the cathedral’s forecourt. This statue quickly became a focal point for the faithful. Two years later, in 1962, Pope John XXIII elevated the church to the status of a basilica, an honor that recognized its importance within the Catholic Church and affirmed its role as a vibrant spiritual center for the local Catholic community. In 2005, reports spread that the statue shed tears, drawing thousands of visitors and pilgrims seeking hope and solace. Whether miracle or coincidence, this moment deepened the statue’s role as a symbol of comfort and unity amid the city’s bustling life. The basilica title brought with it a higher profile in religious ceremonies and pilgrimages, reinforcing the cathedral’s place in both sacred tradition and the everyday rituals of Ho Chi Minh City’s residents. The cathedral’s red bricks, towering spires, and the serene figure of Our Lady of Peace together reveal how this place merges colonial history with ongoing faith and community life. The steady ringing of its bells and the quiet presence of the statue keep time with the city’s changing rhythms, marking this spot as one where history, belief, and daily life intersect.
Directions: From the Notre-Dame Cathedral Basilica of Saigon, stroll down Công xã Paris towards the bustling streets of District 1. Continue straight until you reach the grand Independence Palace, which will be just ahead on Nam Kỳ Khởi Nghĩa. Enjoy the sights along the way!

Stop 2: Independence Palace

Coordinates: 10.7763, 106.6959

Type/Specialty: Historical Site

Specific Examples: The grand reception halls, the war rooms, and the iconic helicopter on the rooftop.

The helicopter pad on the rooftop catches the light, a silent witness to the events it once hosted. Beyond these gates, the streets are alive with commerce and daily life, reflecting the history of the area.

Directions: From Independence Palace, head down Nam Ky Khoi Nghia Street towards the bustling heart of the city. As you stroll, keep an eye out for the beautiful architecture of the Saigon Central Post Office on your right. Continue walking until you reach the lively area around Bến Thành Market, where you can soak in the vibrant atmosphere and explore the stalls. Enjoy the sights and sounds along the way! It is roughly 500 meters away.

Stop 3: Bến Thành Market

Coordinates: 10.772521, 106.698019

Type/Specialty: Market

Specific Examples: Vibrant stalls selling local handicrafts, fresh produce, and street food delicacies.

Orientation: Standing at the broad entrance of this market, your senses encounter a buzzing hive of commerce.

The sheer scale of the market shapes the air itself: a mixture of sizzling food aromas wafts through the open stalls, while the steady shuffle of feet and calls of vendors echo beneath the high ceiling. Here, commerce and the sensory environment intertwine—textures of woven baskets, glass jars, and polished produce display tactile history beneath your fingertips.

Bến Thành Market stands not only as a place of trade but as a dynamic site witnessing the city’s continuous reinvention.
