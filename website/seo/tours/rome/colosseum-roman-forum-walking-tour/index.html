What you'll hear — 5 stops

  1. Roman Forum

    Ancient Roman ruins

    The air is dry with dust, and a breeze stirs weeds that grow between ancient paving stones. Ahead, columns shape a corridor of broken light, and the remains of arches and plinths stretch across the valley floor.

  2. Colosseum

    Ancient amphitheater

    The old Republican city had given way to imperial power, and the unfinished amphitheatre commissioned by Vespasian was inherited and opened for the first time by his son.

  3. Arch of Constantine

    Triumphal arch

    This is not simply a decorative monument—it is the largest Roman triumphal arch, standing 21 meters high and nearly 26 meters wide, with its central passage soaring 11.5 meters above the worn paving stones.

  4. Basilica of Maxentius and Constantine

    Ancient basilica ruins

    Once, this immense structure was the beating heart of civic life.

  5. Curia Julia

    Ancient Senate house

    Tall is almost severe brick façade, beneath the high windows of the Curia Julia. Here, to your left, the Roman Forum opens—a place you have already walked, crowded now as it was then with the voices of Rome.
