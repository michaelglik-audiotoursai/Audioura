What you'll hear — 5 stops

  1. Adorazione dei Magi

    The first shock as you step before Gentile da Fabriano’s "Adorazione dei Magi" in the Uffizi Gallery is the shimmer—stand directly in front of the altarpiece, just off the axis of the gallery’s natural light.

  2. Leda col cigno (Leda and the Swan)

    “Leda col cigno,” now attributed to Giovanni Francesco Melzi, was created in the early 16th century using oil and resin on a wooden panel.

  3. Adamo ed Eva

    By setting his name and the date directly on the surface, Cranach not only marked the work as authentic but also placed himself in direct competition with Italian painters, who were renowned for their mastery of oil…

  4. Annunciazione (The Annunciation)

    In 1867, The Annunciation was housed at the Church in Monte Oliveto, just outside Porta San Frediano in Florence.
