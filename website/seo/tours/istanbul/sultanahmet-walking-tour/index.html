What you'll hear — 5 stops

  1. Hagia Sophia

    Historical Museum

    The present mosque, completed in 537, was built to serve as the Capitol of Christianity in the Eastern Roman Empire.

  2. Basilica Cistern

    Ancient Water Reservoir

    Wooden walkway is just above the surface of the ancient reservoir, where columns rise out of the gloom and the air is cool, touched with the scent of damp stone.

  3. Sultanahmet Square

    Public Square

    In those days, the city was still called Byzantium—a provincial town whose future imperial weight was unimaginable.

  4. Hippodrome of Constantinople

    Historical Site

    The Greek word ‘hippodrome’ reveals its purpose: 'hippos' means horse, 'dromos' means path—a place built for racing, spectacle, and gathering.

  5. Blue Mosque (Sultan Ahmed Mosque)

    Active Mosque

    Blue Mosque (Sultan Ahmed Mosque) — one of the highlights on this tour, with its full story narrated in the Audioura app.

Frequently asked questions

Which is better to visit, Hagia Sophia or the Blue Mosque?

Open the Audioura app, search for this tour, and press play at the first stop. The app guides you from stop to stop by voice, so your phone can stay in your pocket.

How much does an entrance ticket to Hagia Sophia cost?

Audioura provides the audio tour, not venue admission. Entry tickets and opening hours are set by the site itself and can change, so check the official venue before you go — then let Audioura narrate your visit once you're inside.

How long is the Sultanahmet audio tour?

The Sultanahmet tour has 5 stops with about 11 minutes of narration. Because it's self-guided you can pause, linger or pick it up again whenever you like. The stops are spread over roughly a 1 km walk, so your total time depends on how long you linger.
