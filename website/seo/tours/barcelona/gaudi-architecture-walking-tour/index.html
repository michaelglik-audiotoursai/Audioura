Frequently asked questions

What is the best Gaudí building to visit in Barcelona?

Open the Audioura app, search for this tour, and press play at the first stop. The app guides you from stop to stop by voice, so your phone can stay in your pocket.

How long is the Gaudi Architecture audio tour?

The Gaudi Architecture tour has 5 stops with about 11 minutes of narration. Because it's self-guided you can pause, linger or pick it up again whenever you like. The stops are spread over roughly a 4.5 km walk, so your total time depends on how long you linger.

Is this a self-guided tour?

Yes. Audioura is a self-guided audio tour: you set your own pace and the app narrates each stop as you reach it, triggered by GPS or a tap. Triple-click your headphone button to jump between stops without looking at your phone.
