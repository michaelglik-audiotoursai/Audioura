What you'll hear — 5 stops

  1. Barcelona Cathedral

    Gothic Cathedral

    This site has carried layer upon layer of communal memory: after the Moors destroyed the original building in 985, a Romanesque church replaced it in 1085.

  2. Plaça del Rei

    Historical Square

    Plaça del Rei is the angular stone square enclosed by the looming walls of the old royal palace. Plaça del Rei has witnessed both triumph and terror throughout its history.

  3. Carrer del Bisbe

    Historic Street

    The Pont del Bisbe above you is not a relic of the Middle Ages, but a deliberate creation of 1928—a moment when the city decided to tie its present to the visual language of its past.

  4. Palau de la Generalitat de Catalunya

    Government Building

    The Palau de la Generalitat de Catalunya was not always the seat of Catalan government, but in 1931, its role shifted dramatically.

  5. Plaça Sant Jaume

    This is not just a meeting place, but the place where the city’s destinies have been declared for centuries.
