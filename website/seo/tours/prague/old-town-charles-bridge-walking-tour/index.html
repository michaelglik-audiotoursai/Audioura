What you'll hear — 5 stops

  1. Old Town Square

    Historic Square

    Old Town Square is not just a crossroads of Prague’s streets but of its living memory. The Old Town Hall, established in 1338, anchors one side of the square.

  2. Charles Bridge

    Historic Bridge

    Threshold: to your left is the Vltava’s waters move beneath arches of sandstone. The towers at each end mark the boundary between centuries, their stonework darkened by time and rain.

  3. Jan Hus Monument

    Historical Monument

    Erected in 1915, this monument stands as a public commemoration of Jan Hus—a Czech reformer whose mark is made visible here in stone.

  4. Týn Church

    Gothic Church

    The Church of Our Lady before Týn has anchored this part of Prague as its main church since 1385, rising on the site of earlier Romanesque and Gothic buildings.

  5. Astronomical Clock

    Astronomical Clock

    The best view is from the cobbled arc just in front, where the crowd swells each hour to witness its procession. The legend names Master Hanuš, a figure woven into the city’s folklore.
