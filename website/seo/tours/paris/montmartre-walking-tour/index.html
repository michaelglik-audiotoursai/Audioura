What you'll hear — 5 stops

  1. Espace Dalí

    Art Museum

    The façade offers little hint of what lies within: one of Paris’s richest troves of Salvador Dalí’s sculptures and illustrations, just steps from the bustle of Place du Tertre.

  2. Basilique du Sacré-Cœur

    Basilica

    Construction of the Basilique du Sacré-Cœur began in 1875. The building itself was conceived as a response to the shattering events of the Franco-Prussian War and the capture of Napoleon III at the Battle of Sedan.

  3. Place du Tertre

    Public Square

    This square once formed the core of the Benedictine Montmartre Abbey, established in 1133 by King Louis VI.

  4. La Maison Rose

    Restaurant

    This is a corner where the rhythm of Montmartre slows — and yet, in 1908, Germaine Gargallo and her husband, the Catalan painter Ramon Pichot, turned this modest building into a quiet revolution.

  5. Le Bateau-Lavoir

    The façade is unadorned, patched with soot and the wear of decades, but a plaque above the door confirms you have arrived at Le Bateau-Lavoir.
