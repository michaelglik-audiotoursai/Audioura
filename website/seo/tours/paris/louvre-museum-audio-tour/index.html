What you'll hear — 5 stops

  1. La Mort de la Vierge (Death of the Virgin)

    This work was created with a realism that was virtually unprecedented for such a sacred subject.

  2. L'Astronome (toile)

    Johannes Vermeer, a Dutch painter known for light and color, painted “L’Astronome” at a stage in his life when the Dutch Republic was experiencing a surge in scientific and philosophical exploration.

  3. La Dérision du Christ (The Mocking of Christ)

    In 2019, an elderly French lady named Le Goff discovered this painting above her hot plate while sorting through belongings in her modest kitchen.

  4. Victoire de Samothrace (Winged Victory of Samothrace)

    The Winged Victory of Samothrace was created around 190 BCE and is attributed to the School of Rhodes.

  5. La Décollation de saint Jean-Baptiste

    Its scale and subject were chosen for the solemn walls of Notre-Dame, where it would confront congregants with the story of sacrifice and prophecy.
