**Date:** June 2026  
**Goal:** Complete developer account setup and begin App Store Connect organization enrollment  
**Timeline:** Complete by June 25, 2026 (6 days for D-U-N-S verification)

---

## Step 1: Create Apple Developer Account & Set Up 2FA ⭐ (You are here)

### Action 1.1: Create/Sign In to Apple ID

1. Go to: https://developer.apple.com
2. Click **Account** (top right)
3. **If you don't have an Apple ID:**
   - Click **Sign in**
   - Click **Create Apple ID**
   - Email: `glikfamily@gmail.com`
   - Create a strong password (save it)
   - Verify your email (Apple sends a code)
   - Complete account creation
4. **If you already have an Apple ID:**
   - Click **Sign in**
   - Use your existing credentials

**Result:** You're signed into your Apple ID

---

### Action 1.2: Accept Apple Developer Program Terms

After signing in, you'll see a prompt:

**"Accept Apple Developer Program License Agreement"**

1. Read the terms (or skim to the bottom)
2. **Scroll to the bottom** and find the checkbox:
   - ☐ "I have read and agree to the Apple Developer Program License Agreement"
3. **Check the box**
4. Click **Accept** (blue button)

**Note:** This is just the program agreement — not a legal commitment yet. It's Apple's standard ToS for developers.

**Result:** Terms accepted, 2FA setup begins

---

### Action 1.3: Set Up Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Apple **requires** 2FA for all developer accounts. You'll see a screen:

**"Set Up Two-Factor Authentication"**

**Option A: Phone Number (Recommended)**

1. **Enter your phone number:**
   - Country: United States (or your location)
   - Phone: Your mobile number (e.g., +1 555-123-4567)
2. **Choose delivery method:**
   - ☑️ **Text message (SMS)** (faster)
   - ☐ Voice call
3. Click **Send Code**
4. **Apple sends a 6-digit code to your phone**
5. **Enter the code** in the field
6. Click **Verify**

**Result:** 2FA enabled via phone. You can now proceed to the developer program enrollment.

---

### Action 1.4: Verify Your Identity (Optional but Recommended)

After 2FA is set up, Apple may ask:

**"Verify Your Identity"**

This is optional for account creation, but required later for App Store submission. You can:
- Do it now (faster), or
- Skip it and do it during organization enrollment (Step 2)

**If doing it now:**
1. Provide your name, address, date of birth
2. Apple verifies via public records
3. Takes 5-15 minutes

**For now, you can skip this** — we'll do it in Step 2 with your organization details.

---

### Action 1.5: Confirm Account Creation

After 2FA is complete, you should see:

**"Apple Developer Account Created"**

- Confirmation email sent to `glikfamily@gmail.com`
- Check your email for the confirmation link (click it)

**Result:** Your Apple Developer Account is live and ready for the Developer Program enrollment (Step 2)

## Troubleshooting

**Problem:** Can't find the "Accept Terms" button
- **Solution:** Check that you're signed in (Account → shows your email)
- Scroll down to the bottom of the page to find the checkbox and button

**Problem:** 2FA code doesn't arrive via SMS
- **Solution:** Try "Voice call" option instead
- Check your phone's spam/blocked messages

**Problem:** Already have an Apple ID but forgot password
- **Solution:** Go to iforgot.apple.com and reset it
- Use the reset account for your developer account

**Problem:** 2FA asks for a recovery code
- **Solution:** You'll see "Can't access your trusted device?"
- Use your recovery codes (you should have saved these when first setting up 2FA on your Apple ID)
- If you don't have them, click "Didn't receive a code?" and choose a different method
