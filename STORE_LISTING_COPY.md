## Full description  (max 4000)
```
Audioura turns the world around you into something you can listen to. Point it at a place and get an instant, AI-narrated audio tour — or hand it an article or newsletter and have it read to you while you walk, drive, or relax.

TWO WAYS TO LISTEN

Tours
Get a guided audio tour for where you are or where you're headed. Audioura uses your location to find points of interest nearby and generates a narrated tour on the spot — history, context, and stories for landmarks, neighborhoods, museums, and more. Tap a spot on the map, press play, and explore at your own pace.

Audio
Turn reading into listening. Paste a news article or newsletter and Audioura reads it aloud in a clear, natural voice — perfect for your commute, a workout, or whenever your eyes need a break.

WHY YOU'LL LIKE IT
- Instant tours: no pre-downloaded packs — tours are generated on demand for the place you choose.
- Natural narration: clear, easy-to-follow audio.
- Hands-free and eyes-free: built for listening on the move.
- Save and revisit: keep the tours you love.
- No account required: start listening right away.

Audioura is free to use. Whether you're traveling somewhere new, rediscovering your own city, or just catching up on reading by ear, Audioura is your audio guide to the world.
```
