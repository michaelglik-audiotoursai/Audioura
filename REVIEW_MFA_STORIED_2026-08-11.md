## The honest problem: it still tells you *that*, not *why*

Stop 1's story paragraph:

> "**Louis Broder, the publisher**, played a crucial role in bringing this stunning
> work to life… **Mourlot Frères, the printer**, expertly executed Miró's vision…
> **Boris Fridman, the generous donor**, gifted this extraordinary piece."

Three names, three job titles, three empty verbs — "played a crucial role",
"expertly executed", "gifted". A listener learns a publisher published, a printer
printed, a donor donated. **This is the roll-call, not the story.**

Compare what the search *can* now return (verified live at 10:02 today):

> "Picasso met **Fernand Mourlot in October 1945** after much encouragement from

That has a person, a date, a reason, a consequence. **It is not in the tour.**
