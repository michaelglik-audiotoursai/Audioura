1. Stop 1: Gallery NAGA
Address: 67 Newbury St, Boston, MA 02116
Coordinates: 42.3500, -71.0762
Type/Specialty: Contemporary art gallery showcasing emerging and established artists
Specific Examples: Known for their vibrant abstract paintings by local Boston artists, innovative mixed media installations featuring recycled materials, and thought-provoking sculptures that explore urban themes
Operational Details: Open Tuesday-Saturday 10:00 AM - 5:30 PM, Sunday 12:00 PM - 5:00 PM, closed Mondays. Free admission, artworks range from $500 to $15,000. No reservations needed but group tours available by appointment.
Visitor Tips: Allow extra time to fully appreciate the exhibits and consider guided tour options.

Orientation: You can find this location at 67 Newbury St, Boston, MA 02116. For GPS navigation, use coordinates: 42.3500, -71.0762.

Walking Directions: From the nearest T station, Arlington Station on the Green Line, head west on Newbury Street for about 3 blocks. Pass Starbucks and CVS on your right. Gallery NAGA is on the left side in a modern glass-fronted building with large display windows.

2. Stop 2: Panopticon Gallery
Address: 502c Commonwealth Ave, Boston, MA 02215
Coordinates: 42.3495, -71.0955
Type/Specialty: Photography gallery featuring contemporary and vintage photographic works
Specific Examples: Showcases black and white street photography capturing Boston's urban life, iconic portraits of jazz musicians from the 1960s-80s, and stunning New England landscape photographs including Cape Cod seascapes
Operational Details: Open Wednesday-Saturday 11:00 AM - 6:00 PM, Sunday 1:00 PM - 5:00 PM. Free admission, prints available for purchase starting at $200. Located inside Hotel Commonwealth, accessible through main lobby.
Visitor Tips: Allow extra time to fully appreciate the exhibits and consider guided tour options.

Orientation: You can find this location at 502c Commonwealth Ave, Boston, MA 02215. For GPS navigation, use coordinates: 42.3495, -71.0955.

Walking Directions: From Gallery NAGA, head east on Newbury Street for 2 blocks, then turn left onto Massachusetts Avenue. Walk north for 4 blocks, then turn right onto Commonwealth Avenue. Panopticon Gallery is located on the right inside Hotel Commonwealth at the corner of Charlesgate East.

3. Stop 3: Galatea Fine Art
Address: 460 Harrison Ave, #B6, Boston, MA 02118
Coordinates: 42.3420, -71.0665
Type/Specialty: Artist-run contemporary art gallery featuring local and regional talents
Specific Examples: Features colorful abstract paintings inspired by Boston Harbor, intricate mixed media collages incorporating vintage Boston Globe clippings, and experimental sculptures made from reclaimed materials from local construction sites
Operational Details: Open Wednesday-Sunday 12:00 PM - 6:00 PM, closed Monday-Tuesday. Free admission, artworks range from $100 to $8,000. First Friday openings with artist talks at 7:00 PM.
Visitor Tips: Allow extra time to fully appreciate the exhibits and consider guided tour options.

Orientation: You can find this location at 460 Harrison Ave, #B6, Boston, MA 02118. For GPS navigation, use coordinates: 42.3420, -71.0665.

Walking Directions: From Panopticon Gallery, head south on Massachusetts Avenue for 6 blocks to Boylston Street. Turn right on Boylston and walk west for 3 blocks to Tremont Street. Turn left on Tremont, then right on Herald Street, and left on Harrison Avenue. Galatea Fine Art is in the SoWa Art + Design District on the left side, entrance through the courtyard.

TOUR TIPS: Museum tours work well any time of day. Check individual museum hours and consider purchasing tickets in advance for popular exhibitions.
