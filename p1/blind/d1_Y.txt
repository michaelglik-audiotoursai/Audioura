Stop 1: Blue Frog Bakery

Address: 1231 Washington Street, Jamaica Plain, Massachusetts, 02130.

Coordinates: 42.3201, -71.1053

Type/Specialty: Bakery/Coffee Shop

Specific Examples: Freshly baked pastries, artisan coffee, cozy seating.

Orientation: You are about to embark on a walking journey through coffee shops in Jamaica Plain, Boston. In Jamaica Plain, coffee shops are not just places to sip a brew; they are stories of community and resilience. Your first stop is Blue Frog Bakery. From the Forest Hills T station, head northwest on Washington St for about 0.3 miles, and you'll find Blue Frog Bakery on your left. You'll notice the vibrant murals on the buildings as you walk. Sunlight glints off the hand-lettered sign by Josh Luke of Best Dressed Signs, its cheerful frog marking the spot where locals queue for a morning treat.

Blue Frog Bakery is more than just a place for flour and sugar—it’s a story of a leap across borders. Brad Brown, the baker whose name is well known in these parts, came to Boston from Canada. He arrived with a talent for scones and croissants, and the persistence needed to start over in a new place. Brad founded Blue Frog Bakery here in Jamaica Plain, trading old routines for early mornings and taking the risk of opening a neighborhood bakery from scratch.

One episode that regulars still mention with a grin happened in March 2019, when the bakery marked fifteen years since Brad first warmed the ovens on this block. For the anniversary, he threw a party with special pastries crafted just for the occasion, drawing in everyone from old-timers to newcomers. The bakery was packed, the glass cases filled with creations you couldn’t find on any other day. That morning was more than nostalgia—it was a sense of community, built loaf by loaf.

Another turning point came in 2015, when Brad decided to expand beyond these walls and announced plans to open La Rana Rossa Pizzeria in the old Cafe Bartlett Square. This decision showed not just ambition, but a commitment to growing the neighborhood’s food scene, spreading the same hands-on approach that made Blue Frog a local anchor.

The hum of conversation inside Blue Frog Bakery carries these histories—immigration, reinvention, and celebration, all marked in pastries and flour. The bakery is open from 7:30 am to 4:00 pm, Tuesday through Sunday, with Monday as the one day the ovens rest. No bookings are needed.

Directions: As you stroll, keep an eye out for the beautiful Jamaica Pond on your left. Continue walking until you reach the corner where you’ll find Ula Cafe, just a short walk past the intersection with South Street.

Stop 2: Ula Cafe

Address:

Coordinates: 42.3206, -71.1042

Type/Specialty: Cafe

Specific Examples: Locally sourced ingredients, specialty coffee drinks, communal tables.

Orientation: Standing just across from the bustle of Amory Street, the corner windows of Ula Cafe catch the morning light. This is the best vantage to watch the flow of locals ducking in for coffee or a midday sandwich.

Ula Cafe’s story begins in 2005—a date marked by the opening of its doors as the neighborhood’s appetite for a true community gathering place came to life. The aroma rising here mingles with the scent from the Sam Adams Brewery, tying today’s scene to the long tradition of food and drink that shaped Jamaica Plain. The brewery itself has a long history in the neighborhood, and its presence just steps away continues to feed the area’s creative, energetic feel. A pivotal moment in Ula Cafe’s history arrived in May 2015. That month, the cafe expanded its menu to include beer, joining the neighborhood’s brewing legacy and shifting its role from morning coffee stop to afternoon retreat. The introduction of beer brought in a new crowd and fostered looser, longer conversations—neighbors lingering over a pint where before they might have rushed out with a pastry. The decision to add beer was not just about variety; it connected Ula Cafe more deeply to the pulse of Jamaica Plain, where the boundary between workday and leisure blurs across the café’s wooden tables. As you decide whether to step inside, know that Ula Cafe opens its doors from 8:00 am to 3:00 pm on Monday and Tuesday. The stop you visited just before, Blue Frog Bakery, gave you a taste of sweet tradition; here at Ula, the living rhythm of the neighborhood continues—one mug, one meal, one conversation at a time. The next aroma on our tour will carry you to a place where tradition meets innovation.

Directions: From Ula Cafe, head down South Street towards the intersection with Centre Street. As you stroll along, you’ll pass by the lovely green spaces of Jamaica Pond on your right. Continue along Centre Street, and soon you’ll spot Fiore's Bakery on your left, just a little past the colorful storefronts. Enjoy the sweet treats waiting for you! It is roughly 150 meters away.
