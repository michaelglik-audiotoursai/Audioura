Regina Pizzeria specializes in Italian food, with a focus on brick-oven, thin-crust pizzas that pull regulars and newcomers alike. Regina has been shaping Boston’s pizza scene since before most locals’ grandparents were born. By the 1950s, the North End was changing, but Regina’s brick oven stayed hot. The Polcari family took over in 1956, bringing new energy and recipes. Since then, Regina’s has remained family-run, and their stewardship turned this single shop into the best-known outpost of New England’s own pizza chain. The Polcaris didn’t just keep the place running; they held tight to tradition while letting the menu evolve ever so slightly.
