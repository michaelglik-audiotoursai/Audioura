Stop 1: Nu bleu IV

Address: 164 Avenue des Arènes de Cimiez, 06000 Nice, France

Coordinates: 43.7196, 7.2755

Museum Information: Closed on Tuesday. 10:00–17:00 (1 Nov–31 Mar); 10:00–18:00 (1 Apr–31 Oct). €12; free for Métropole residents

You are about to embark on a journey through the chapters of Henri Matisse's legacy at the Musée Matisse. Inaugurated in 1963 within the elegant Villa des Arènes in Cimiez, this museum is a living testament to Matisse's dedication to preserving his artistic journey. Explore how his pioneering cut-out techniques, generously donated to the museum, showcase his late creative evolution. Delve into the serene power of Matisse's paintings, a reflection of his time in Nice, gifted to the museum as a visionary contribution. Uncover the mystery behind the museum's growth, from its inception to the landmark acquisition in 2025, as you unravel the Evolution of Matisse Museum's Collection.

As you gaze upon Nu bleu IV, created by Henri Matisse in 1952, you are met with a stunning depiction of a female nude rendered in blue gouache-covered paper. This unique medium is cut and pasted on white Canson paper, mounted on a vertical canvas, creating a dynamic interplay of color and texture. The composition exudes a sense of fluidity and grace, capturing the essence of the human form with a striking use of color and form. Matisse's choice of the color blue, known for its calming and introspective qualities, adds a layer of depth to the piece, inviting the viewer to contemplate the emotional resonance of the artwork. The artist's mastery of cut-out techniques is on full display, showcasing his late creative evolution and dedication to pushing the boundaries of traditional artistic expression. One cannot fully appreciate Nu bleu IV without understanding its historical significance as the first and last Blue Nude completed by Matisse. This fact underscores the profound journey of creation and exploration that the artist embarked upon, culminating in this masterpiece that now graces the walls of the Musée Matisse. Nu bleu IV stands as a testament to Matisse's enduring legacy and his generous spirit, as it was donated to the museum in 1979 and has been on loan since 1989. This artwork is a jewel in the museum's collection, symbolizing the evolution of Matisse's artistic vision and the lasting impact of his contributions to the world of art. As you immerse yourself in the beauty of Nu bleu IV, consider the treasures that Matisse's generosity has brought to this artistic sanctuary.

Stop 2: Nymphe dans la forêt

Address: 164 Avenue des Arènes de Cimiez, 06000 Nice, France

Henri Matisse's "Nymphe dans la forêt" transports viewers into a realm where myth and nature intertwine seamlessly. Created between 1936 and 1938, this painting showcases a departure from Matisse's typical style, embracing a narrative rich in symbolism and movement. The scene unfolds with a nude nymph, her form gracefully reclining amidst verdant foliage, while a satyr, a symbol of untamed desire, approaches her with a sense of intrigue. Matisse's masterful brushwork is evident in the dynamic composition that captures the tension between the two figures. The colors, a harmonious blend of earthy tones and vibrant accents, evoke a sense of enchantment and mystery. The contrast between light and shadow adds depth to the scene, emphasizing the ethereal quality of the forest setting. Embedded within this mythical encounter is a reflection of Matisse's exploration of primal instincts and the allure of the natural world. The juxtaposition of the delicate nymph and the robust satyr speaks to themes of duality and primal desires, inviting viewers to contemplate the complexities of human nature. As you contemplate "Nymphe dans la forêt," consider how Matisse's visionary approach to storytelling through art transcends time and invites us to delve into the depths of myth and emotion. This painting, now housed at the Musée Matisse in Nice, stands as a testament to the artist's ability to capture the essence of ancient tales and infuse them with a modern sensibility. In experiencing this work, one can truly appreciate the serene power of Matisse's creations and the enduring legacy he has left within the walls of this museum.
