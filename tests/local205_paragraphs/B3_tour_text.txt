Stop 1: Nu bleu IV

Address: 164 Avenue des Arènes de Cimiez, 06000 Nice, France

Coordinates: 43.7196, 7.2755

Museum Information: Closed on Tuesday. 10:00–17:00 (1 Nov–31 Mar); 10:00–18:00 (1 Apr–31 Oct). €12; free for Métropole residents

Embark on a journey through the chapters of Henri Matisse's Legacy and Donations at the Musée Matisse in Nice. As you step into the Villa des Arènes, a seventeenth-century gem in Cimiez, you delve into Matisse's world. Witness the unveiling of his innovative Blue Nudes series, a testament to his mastery of the cut-out technique. Each piece echoes his deep connection to nature, culminating in a profound merging of art and environment. Discover how Matisse's personal donations, alongside contributions from his heirs, have intricately woven the tapestry of this museum's collection. From its inauguration in 1963 to the recent 2025 donation of "Nature morte à la statuette africaine," every brushstroke and sculpture speaks of Matisse's enduring legacy. Join us as we unravel the enigmatic tale of artistic devotion and familial heritage within these hallowed walls.

As you gaze upon Nu bleu IV, created in 1952 by the renowned French painter Henri Matisse, you are captivated by the unique technique employed in this artwork. The piece is a female nude crafted from blue gouache-covered paper, meticulously cut and pasted onto white Canson paper, all mounted on a vertical canvas. This innovative approach showcases Matisse's mastery in exploring form and color through a combination of materials, inviting you to delve into the intricate layers of his creation. One notable aspect of Nu bleu IV is its place within Matisse's Blue Nudes series, marking the beginning and end of this collection. This particular piece holds the distinction of being the first Blue Nude that Matisse started and the last one he completed, adding a profound layer of significance to its presence in the museum. Contextualizing this artwork within the broader collection at Musée Matisse, you can trace Henri Matisse's legacy through his generous donations to the museum, which include 68 paintings and gouaches. Additionally, the museum features contributions from the heirs of Matisse, highlighting the enduring impact of the artist's work on the institution. Nu bleu IV not only stands as a testament to Matisse's artistic evolution but also prompts reflection on how his contributions evolved over time. This piece serves as a gateway to exploring the breadth of Matisse's oeuvre and the influence he continues to exert on the museum's collection. As you ponder the progression of Matisse's artistry, consider the lasting imprint he has left on the Musée Matisse and the world of modern art.

Stop 2: Nymphe dans la forêt

Address: 164 Avenue des Arènes de Cimiez, 06000 Nice, France

In the heart of Musée Matisse, a captivating painting by Henri Matisse beckons with a departure from his usual style. "Nymphe dans la forêt," created between 1936 and 1938, portrays a mythological scene of a naked nymph in a forest, being approached by a satyr. The oil on canvas work, currently on deposit here, showcases Matisse's exploration of a different theme, merging his love for nature with his artistic evolution. What draws the eye is Matisse's bold use of color and form, capturing the essence of the mythical encounter with a modern twist. The sinuous lines and vibrant hues dance across the canvas, inviting viewers into a realm where myth and reality intertwine. This departure from his traditional style signifies a pivotal moment in Matisse's oeuvre, showcasing his versatility and willingness to push artistic boundaries. A piece of historical context that enriches the interpretation of this painting is its display at the Matisse. Cahiers d'art – Le tournant des années 1930 exhibition at the musée de l'Orangerie in 2023. This exposure highlighted Matisse's continued relevance and innovation in the art world, cementing his legacy as a visionary artist. As part of the broader collection at Musée Matisse, "Nymphe dans la forêt" stands as a testament to Matisse's artistic prowess and the evolution of his style over time. This painting exemplifies the rich tapestry of themes and influences that define Matisse's legacy, connecting visitors to the artist's creative journey and the museum's commitment to preserving his contributions to the art world. Immerse yourself in the enchanting world of Matisse's "Nymphe dans la forêt," where myth and artistry converge to evoke a sense of awe and wonder.
