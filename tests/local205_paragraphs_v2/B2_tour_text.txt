Step-by-Step Audio Guided Tour: Musée Matisse, Nice, France - Museum Tour
Tour-Category: museum

Stop 1: Nymphe dans la forêt

Address: 164 Avenue des Arènes de Cimiez, 06000 Nice, France

Coordinates: 43.7196, 7.2755

Museum Information: Closed on Tuesday. 10:00–17:00 (1 Nov–31 Mar); 10:00–18:00 (1 Apr–31 Oct). €12; free for Métropole residents

Orientation: Stand at the center of the gallery, bathed in the soft glow of natural light filtering through the large windows. Position yourself directly in front of "Nymphe dans la forêt" to fully immerse yourself in the mystical ambiance crafted by Henri Matisse.

You are about to embark on a journey through the chapters of donations shaping the collection of the Musée Matisse. Established in 1963 within the historic Villa des Arènes in Cimiez, this museum holds a treasure trove of Matisse's creations. Begin by delving into the personal donations of Matisse himself, showcasing his profound ties to Nice, where he resided and worked for decades. The narrative unfolds to a climactic moment in 2025, marked by the pivotal donation of 'Nature morte à la statuette africaine,' the first painted masterpiece by Matisse to grace the museum since its inception. As you explore the evolution and expansion of the museum, you unravel the mystery of how these generous contributions have shaped its rich tapestry of art.

Step into the enchanting world of "Nymphe dans la forêt," a captivating masterpiece that transports you to a realm where myth and reality intertwine. This oil on canvas painting, created by Matisse between 1936 and 1938, portrays a nude nymph reclining in a lush forest setting. As you gaze upon the scene, notice the delicate interplay of light and shadow that Matisse expertly employs to evoke a sense of ethereal beauty. One striking aspect of this artwork is Matisse's bold use of color and form to convey emotion and depth. The vibrant hues of green, blue, and earth tones harmonize to create a rich tapestry of visual delight. The fluid lines and expressive brushwork capture the essence of movement and tranquility, inviting you to explore the hidden depths of the forest alongside the nymph. To fully appreciate "Nymphe dans la forêt," consider the historical context of Matisse's career and the cultural significance of the nymph and satyr motif in art history. The painting's mythological theme adds layers of symbolism, hinting at themes of sensuality, nature, and the human connection to the divine. As you admire this masterpiece, reflect on the profound impact of Matisse's personal donations, which laid the foundation for the Musée Matisse's diverse collection. Through his deep ties to Nice, where he lived and worked for many years, Matisse's legacy continues to shape the museum's identity and offerings. Explore how later donations have further enriched the museum's collection, weaving a narrative of artistic evolution and cultural exploration that beckons visitors to delve deeper into the world of Matisse and his contemporaries.


Directions: Continue through Musée Matisse — next is Tempête à Nice.



Stop 2: Tempête à Nice

Address: 164 Avenue des Arènes de Cimiez, 06000 Nice, France

As you gaze upon "Tempête à Nice," painted between 1919-1920, the coastal city of Nice during a storm comes alive on the canvas. Matisse, known for his bold colors and innovative approach to form, masterfully depicts the scene from a hotel balcony, offering a dynamic perspective of the crashing waves and turbulent sky. The artist's choice of oil on canvas allows for a rich depth of color and texture, enhancing the dramatic effect of the storm. In this work, Matisse's exploration of light and movement is particularly striking, as he skillfully captures the play of shadows and reflections amidst the storm's fury. The swirling brushstrokes and intense contrasts evoke a sense of energy and motion, inviting viewers to experience the raw power of nature through the artist's eyes. To fully appreciate "Tempête à Nice," it is essential to consider the historical context of the French Riviera during Matisse's time. The region's vibrant atmosphere and changing light served as a constant inspiration for the artist, influencing his bold color choices and expressive style. This painting stands as a testament to Matisse's ability to translate his emotional response to the world around him onto the canvas, creating a dynamic and evocative visual narrative. As you immerse yourself in the stormy seascape of "Tempête à Nice," you are witnessing a pivotal moment in Matisse's artistic journey, where nature and emotion converge in a powerful and timeless expression. This work not only enriches the Musée Matisse's collection but also offers a profound glimpse into the artist's mastery of capturing the essence of a moment in time.



From Nymphe dans la forêt to Tempête à Nice, you have followed the thread of Donations Shaping the Museum's Collection. In 1989, the archaeological museum was moved, allowing the Musée Matisse to be expanded.
