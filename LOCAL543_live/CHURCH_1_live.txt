Orientation: As you stand in the sanctuary, look around at the intricate details of this sacred space. Notice the way the light filters through the stained glass, casting colorful patterns on the wooden pews.

In 2002, this very sanctuary became a focal point for a gathering of over 850 parishioners who assembled to support Father Walter H. Cuenin. His homily that day was a bold declaration against Cardinal Bernard Law, accusing him of hiding behind legalities and financial maneuvering. This moment of defiance contributed to Cardinal Law's resignation and departure to Rome, marking a significant turning point in the church's history and highlighting the sanctuary's role as a catalyst for change. Fast forward to June 2023, the sanctuary was again the center of attention, this time under tragic circumstances. Bruno and Gilda 'Jill' D’Amore were set to renew their marriage vows here for their 50th wedding anniversary. However, they were found murdered in their home just days before the ceremony. Together, they prayed for the D’Amore family and the parish community, reinforcing the sanctuary's role as a place of collective mourning and spiritual refuge. The very name of the church, Our Lady Help of Christians, carries historical weight. This title of the Blessed Virgin Mary, first described by John Chrysostom in AD 345, gained prominence during the Middle Ages. It was notably invoked in 1571 when Pope Pius V attributed the victory of Christian armies over the Ottoman Empire to Mary's intercession. This title symbolizes protection and hope, themes that resonate deeply within the sanctuary's walls.
