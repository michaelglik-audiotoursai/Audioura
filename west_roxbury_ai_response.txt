1. Stop 1: The Corrib Pub
Address: 2030 Centre St, West Roxbury, MA 02132
Coordinates: 42.2796, -71.1708
Type/Specialty: Traditional Irish pub serving hearty comfort food and craft beers
Specific Examples: Famous for their fish and chips with beer-battered cod, shepherd's pie with ground lamb and creamy mashed potatoes, and extensive selection of Irish whiskeys and local craft beers
Operational Details: Open Monday-Thursday 11:30 AM - 12:00 AM, Friday-Saturday 11:30 AM - 1:00 AM, Sunday 12:00 PM - 12:00 AM. Entrees range from $12-24, full bar available, reservations recommended for weekend dinners
Walking Directions: From the West Roxbury T station on the Orange Line, head south on Centre Street for about 4 blocks. The Corrib Pub is on the right side with distinctive green awning and outdoor seating area.

2. Stop 2: Himalayan Bistro
Address: 1735 Centre St, West Roxbury, MA 02132
Coordinates: 42.2825, -71.1695
Type/Specialty: Authentic Nepalese and Indian restaurant featuring traditional spices and cooking methods
Specific Examples: Specializes in momos (Nepalese dumplings) with chicken or vegetable filling, aromatic chicken tikka masala with basmati rice, and dal bhat (traditional Nepalese lentil curry with rice)
Operational Details: Open Tuesday-Sunday 5:00 PM - 9:30 PM, closed Mondays. Entrees range from $14-22, BYOB restaurant, takeout and delivery available, vegetarian and vegan options clearly marked
Walking Directions: From The Corrib Pub, head north on Centre Street for 3 blocks. Himalayan Bistro is on the left side in a small plaza with ample parking.

3. Stop 3: Rox Diner
Address: 1884 Centre St, West Roxbury, MA 02132
Coordinates: 42.2810, -71.1702
Type/Specialty: Classic American diner serving breakfast all day and comfort food favorites
Specific Examples: Known for their fluffy buttermilk pancakes with real maple syrup, loaded breakfast platters with eggs, bacon, and home fries, and homemade meatloaf with mashed potatoes and gravy
Operational Details: Open daily 6:00 AM - 3:00 PM. Breakfast items $6-12, lunch entrees $8-16, cash and credit accepted, counter seating and booths available, popular weekend brunch spot
Walking Directions: From Himalayan Bistro, continue north on Centre Street for 2 blocks, then cross to the right side. Rox Diner is in a standalone building with classic diner architecture and neon signage.
