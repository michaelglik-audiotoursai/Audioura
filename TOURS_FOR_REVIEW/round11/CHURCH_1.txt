The stained glass windows of Our Lady Help of Christians Catholic Church hold an intriguing past. Unlike the traditional stained glass that decorates many other churches, these windows were revealed during a restoration to be a trompe-l’œil—a technique that uses painted illusions instead of actual colored glass. This discovery was made by conservators who were examining the windows as part of a broader restoration effort. The trompe-l’œil technique, which means "deceive the eye" in French, creates the illusion of stained glass without the extensive labor and expense typically required. This approach speaks volumes about the creativity and resourcefulness of the artists who aimed to bring beauty and spirituality into this sacred space even when resources were constrained.
