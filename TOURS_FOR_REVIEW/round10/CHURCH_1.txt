Stop 1: Nave

Address: 573 Washington St, Newton, MA 02458

Coordinates: 42.354062, -71.193292

Type/Specialty: Church interior

Specific Examples: High ceilings, wooden pews, religious artwork

Operational Details: Open for prayer and services, check website for schedule

Orientation: You are about to embark on a walking journey through Our Lady Help of Christians Catholic Church, Newton MA. From the serene Nave to the intimate Side Chapels, you'll explore the spiritual heart of this architectural gem. Designed by Jean Bazaine, the stained-glass windows depict the seven sacraments, while the altar, crafted by Eric Boeglin and Deacon Jim, stands as a testament to dedication. Founded in 1868, Mary Help of Christians whispers tales of parishioners from years past, painting a vivid picture of the spiritual life that once thrived within these walls. At the upcoming stops, you will learn about the financial misappropriation controversy at the Nave and the community activism surrounding the Side Chapels in late 2023 and early 2024. Your first stop is Nave. Exit the main entrance and turn left, walk straight through the vestibule to enter the nave. Position yourself to best view this location.

In this sacred space, a significant chapter unfolded on December 8, 2002. More than 850 parishioners gathered here and twice rose to their feet, offering standing ovations to Rev. Walter H. Cuenin. During his homily, Cuenin boldly criticized Cardinal Bernard Law, addressing the broader scandal that had enveloped the church. This moment resonated beyond the nave, highlighting the power of the pulpit as a platform for truth and accountability within the community. Rev. Cuenin's journey at Our Lady Help of Christians took a dramatic turn in September 2005. Allegations of financial misappropriation forced him to resign, a decision that sparked protests and accusations of a 'hit job' by the archdiocese. This event laid bare the complex dynamics between the church's leadership and its congregation, revealing layers of faith, politics, and community ties that have shaped the church's narrative. In a more recent chapter, the nave became a sanctuary of solace on June 27, 2023. This gathering reflected the church's role as a cornerstone of the community, where collective grief transformed into a testament of shared strength and hope. As you move forward, let the sunlight filtering through the stained glass windows ahead illuminate these stories of resilience and unity etched into the very heart of this church.


Directions: As you exit the Nave, head towards the altar and then walk past the Baptismal font. Continue straight towards the back of the church until you reach the Transept. Once there, turn left and stroll down the aisle until you reach the breathtaking Stained Glass Windows on your right.



Stop 2: Stained Glass Windows

Address: 573 Washington St, Newton, MA 02458

Coordinates: 42.352466, -71.206212

Type/Specialty: Artistic feature

Specific Examples: Colorful depictions of biblical scenes, intricate designs

Operational Details: Visible during church hours, best viewed in natural light

Orientation: Standing before the stained glass windows of Our Lady Help of Christians Catholic Church in Newton, MA, you find yourself bathed in a kaleidoscope of colors. The windows are best viewed from the upper sanctuary, where the light filters through the intricate designs, casting vibrant patterns on the church's interior.

In the 1870s, Father Michael M. Green made a significant decision. He resigned his post in Watertown to oversee the church's construction full-time, a dedication mirrored by his parishioners who tirelessly dug the basement foundation by hand. These immigrant workers, toiling after long shifts at local mills and factories, laid the groundwork for what would become a sanctuary of stained glass storytelling. Each pane tells a tale, not just of biblical scenes but also of the community that brought them to life. During the church's modern restorations by John Canning & Co., artisans uncovered the intricate designs that emulate the light-filtering beauty of stained glass, revealing the dedication of both the original creators and the restorers who sought to preserve this legacy. The windows themselves were crafted by the famed F.X. Zettler Studios in Germany, known for their Munich-style glass. This connection to European craftsmanship underscores the church's cultural bridge between the old world and the new. The windows at Mary Immaculate of Lourdes, another church in the area, were similarly adorned, highlighting a regional affinity for this artistic tradition. As you stand here, reflecting on the historical and artistic journey encapsulated in these windows, consider the labor and artistry that have transcended time. Just beyond the colored light, the altar awaits, its own stories etched in the annals of this sacred space.


Directions: As you stand by the beautiful Stained Glass Windows, walk straight down the center aisle towards the Altar. Pass the rows of pews and notice the intricate details of the church interior as you approach the Altar. You'll be captivated by the serene atmosphere as you continue on foot towards the focal point of Our Lady Help of Christians Catholic Church.



Stop 3: Altar

Address: 573 Washington St, Newton, MA 02458

Coordinates: 42.3499, -71.2047

Type/Specialty: Central worship area

Specific Examples: Ornate decorations, religious symbols, candles

Operational Details: Accessible during services, may be restricted during events

Orientation: As you stand before the altar of Our Lady Help of Christians Catholic Church in Newton, MA, let your eyes trace the craftsmanship of local artisans Eric Boeglin and Deacon Jim. This altar, both a spiritual and artistic centerpiece, speaks volumes of the community's dedication and skill.

The altar’s creation in this sacred space, where you earlier admired the stained glass windows, reflects a tapestry of local craftsmanship. Boeglin and Deacon Jim, using native materials, have imbued this piece with a tangible sense of place and purpose. The wood's grain and the stone's texture echo the landscape of Newton, grounding the altar in its environment. The dedication and blessing of this altar marked a moment of renewed devotion for the parishioners, aligning with the church’s mission under its revered title, "Our Lady Help of Christians." This title, first described by John Chrysostom in AD 345, has deep historical roots, symbolizing protection and guidance for Christian communities across Europe and beyond. In 1995, the church experienced a significant moment when Mother Teresa of Calcutta visited, drawing thousands to the Nonantum neighborhood. This visit not only filled the church with the faithful but also with a profound sense of connection to global acts of charity and compassion that Mother Teresa embodied. Her visit remains a cherished memory, reinforcing the church's mission of service and support. On June 25, 2023, Bruno D’Amore, his wife Gilda “Jill” D’Amore, and her mother Lucia Arpino were killed inside their home in Newton, Massachusetts, having suffered multiple stab wounds and blunt force trauma during an early morning break-in. During this time, the altar stood at the heart of an emotional 'Mass of Peace.' It was here that the community gathered, united in grief yet hopeful for healing. This Mass, steeped in collective mourning, underscored the altar's role as a focal point for both communal and personal solace. As you take in these layers of history and devotion, the altar stands not only as a physical structure but as a symbol of the enduring faith and resilience of this vibrant community.


Directions: As you stand at the Altar, head towards the rear of the church. Walk past the main aisle and continue towards the left side of the building. You'll find the Side Chapels nestled in that corner, marked by beautiful stained glass windows along the way. Just a short stroll from the Altar!



Stop 4: Side Chapels

Address: 573 Washington St, Newton, MA 02458

Coordinates: 42.352625, -71.208890

Type/Specialty: Small devotional spaces

Specific Examples: Statues of saints, prayer kneelers, votive candles

Operational Details: Open for private prayer, check for any ongoing services

Orientation: As you step into the side chapels of Our Lady Help of Christians Catholic Church, the contrast from the grand nave you visited earlier is striking. These intimate spaces offer a quieter, more personal experience. Here, the polished wood of the pews and the gentle flicker of votive candles invite reflection.

In these chapels, the stories of community life unfold in personal and poignant ways. One such story begins in 2002, when Rev. Walter H. Cuenin publicly challenged Cardinal Bernard Law during the clergy abuse scandal affecting the Archdiocese of Boston. This act of courage resulted in a formal ban on the parish by Cardinal Law, marking a significant moment in the church’s history. Cuenin's bold stance resonated deeply within the community, highlighting the church's role as a place of both spiritual refuge and social advocacy. The church opened its doors to shelter approximately 30 migrant families. This compassionate act was not without its challenges, as it sparked rumors of violence and necessitated the hiring of private security to protect the families from outside protesters. This modern chapter in the church's history underscores its enduring role as a sanctuary amidst contemporary social issues. The side chapels, though smaller than the main altar, hold within them the echoes of these stories—of courage, resilience, and community care.
