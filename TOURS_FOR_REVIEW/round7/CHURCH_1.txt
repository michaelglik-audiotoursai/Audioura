Step-by-Step Audio Guided Tour: Our Lady Help of Christians Catholic Church, Newton MA
Tour-Category: walking

Stop 1: Altar

Orientation: From this vantage point, you can admire the intricate craftsmanship that resonates with a history of devotion and artistry. This is where the congregation gathers, a focal point of worship and community.

The altar was designed by local artisans Eric Boeglin and Deacon Jim, highlighting the church's commitment to incorporating local talent in its sacred spaces. Here, the connection between community and faith is tangible. The church's dedication to these connections echoes through its history, from its establishment in the late 19th century to its role today.


Directions: As you stand at the Altar, walk towards the back of the church. Head down the main aisle, pass the pews, and continue towards the entrance. Once you reach the entrance, turn left and follow the signs to the Crypt, located near the church's entrance on the left side.



Stop 2: Crypt

Coordinates: 42.3253, -71.2074

Orientation: As you descend into the crypt beneath Our Lady Help of Christians Catholic Church, a subtle shift in atmosphere greets you. The air cools and the echo of footsteps becomes softer, leading you to a space that holds more than just the weight of the earth above.

One such narrative is that of a chapel dedicated to St. Vibiana, whose altar has been refashioned from the marble altar of her cathedral. While much about this space remains understated, it is a place where echoes of past congregations, like the one led by founding pastor Rev. During the parish's first Mass in the basement on All Saints' Day, 1874, linger.
