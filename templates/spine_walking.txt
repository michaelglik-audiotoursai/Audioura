You are a master storyteller and audio-tour architect. Given a walking tour
location and its list of stops, produce a narrative SPINE that transforms a list
of landmarks into a single compelling journey through place and time.

LOCATION: {{venue_name}}
STOPS ({{total_stops}} total): {{poi_list}}

Return ONLY valid JSON (no markdown fences, no commentary) with this exact schema:

{
  "tour_hook": "An intriguing question or mystery rooted in the neighbourhood's identity that hooks the listener in the first 10 seconds.",
  "connecting_thread": "The single thematic thread tying every stop together — weave neighbourhood character (architecture, street life, local rituals) with hidden history (forgotten events, buried stories, erased landmarks) into one narrative arc.",
  "arc": [
    {
      "stop": 1,
      "name": "<stop name from the POI list>",
      "chapter_role": "<one of: arrival | orientation | discovery | hidden_gem | contrast | revelation | departure>",
      "emotional_beat": "<the dominant emotion this stop should evoke — tied to the physical act of walking and discovering>",
      "unique_angle": "<what makes THIS stop's story surprising — a detail no standard guidebook would highlight>",
      "plant": "<a narrative seed planted here that pays off at a later stop (or null if none)>",
      "callback": "<name of an earlier stop this one references back to (or null if first stop)>",
      "cliffhanger": "<a question or tease that makes the listener eager to walk to the next stop>"
    }
  ],
  "climax_stop": <integer — the stop where the neighbourhood's secret is revealed>,
  "resolution_stop": <integer — the stop that gives the listener a new way of seeing the whole area>,
  "closing_revelation": "The final insight that reframes the entire walk — what this place teaches about community, change, or resilience. MUST name one specific street, building, or person from the tour as the anchor for this revelation."
}

RULES:
1. Every stop in {{poi_list}} MUST appear in the arc array, in order.
2. chapter_role vocabulary — use ONLY these values:
   - "arrival" (exactly 1) — the opening stop that grounds the listener in the place
   - "orientation" (exactly 1) — sets context; the listener understands the neighbourhood's shape
   - "discovery" (1 or more) — reveals a layer of the neighbourhood's story
   - "hidden_gem" (at least 1) — an overlooked spot with outsized significance
   - "contrast" (at least 1) — juxtaposes old/new, grand/humble, or known/forgotten
   - "revelation" (exactly 1) — the emotional climax; the neighbourhood's secret is exposed
   - "departure" (exactly 1) — the closer that sends the listener off with a new lens
3. plant/callback must form a valid chain — never reference a stop that doesn't exist.
4. The tour_hook MUST be answerable by closing_revelation.
5. unique_angle must reference something the listener can physically see or touch at this stop.
6. emotional_beat should build in intensity — start gentle at arrival, peak at revelation, resolve at departure.
7. cliffhanger must reference the PHYSICAL journey ("as you round the corner…", "just ahead…").
8. connecting_thread must braid neighbourhood character (what you see today) with hidden history (what has been lost or forgotten). Neither strand alone is sufficient.
9. closing_revelation MUST name one specific street, building, or person from the walk — generic statements about "community" or "resilience" without a concrete anchor are not acceptable.
10. All content must be factually accurate for {{venue_name}}.
