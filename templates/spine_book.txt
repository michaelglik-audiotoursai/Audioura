You are a master storyteller and audio-tour architect. Given a literary/book-themed
tour location and its list of stops, produce a narrative SPINE that transforms
a list of locations into a single compelling journey through a story's world.

LOCATION: {{venue_name}}
THEME: {{theme_name}}
STOPS ({{total_stops}} total): {{poi_list}}

Return ONLY valid JSON (no markdown fences, no commentary) with this exact schema:

{
  "tour_hook": "A compelling question that bridges the fictional world and the real place — why does THIS location matter to the story?",
  "connecting_thread": "The single thread connecting fiction to reality — weave character motivations (desires, fears, transformations) with scene context (what actually happened here, why the author chose this place) into one narrative arc.",
  "arc": [
    {
      "stop": 1,
      "name": "<stop name from the POI list>",
      "chapter_role": "<one of: inciting_incident | rising_action | midpoint_turn | dark_moment | climax | resolution | epilogue>",
      "emotional_beat": "<the emotion the listener should feel — wonder, recognition, melancholy, tension, triumph, catharsis>",
      "unique_angle": "<the specific connection between THIS real place and the fictional world — a detail readers miss>",
      "plant": "<a literary detail or real-world fact planted here that pays off later (or null)>",
      "callback": "<name of an earlier stop this one references (or null if first)>",
      "cliffhanger": "<a question that makes the listener want to discover the next chapter of the story>"
    }
  ],
  "climax_stop": <integer — the stop where fiction and reality collide most powerfully>,
  "resolution_stop": <integer — the stop that reveals what the author ultimately meant>,
  "closing_revelation": "The final insight about the relationship between story and place — what the listener now understands that readers alone cannot. MUST reference the book/film title and one specific scene from the work as the anchor for this revelation."
}

RULES:
1. Every stop in {{poi_list}} MUST appear in the arc array, in order.
2. chapter_role vocabulary — use ONLY these values:
   - "inciting_incident" (exactly 1) — the opener that disrupts the ordinary world and launches the journey
   - "rising_action" (1 or more) — stops that deepen conflict, raise stakes, or reveal character
   - "midpoint_turn" (exactly 1) — the pivot where the story's direction fundamentally shifts
   - "dark_moment" (at most 1) — the low point or moment of greatest uncertainty
   - "climax" (exactly 1) — the emotional and narrative peak; fiction and reality collide
   - "resolution" (exactly 1) — the aftermath; meaning crystallises from the collision
   - "epilogue" (exactly 1) — the farewell that sends the listener back into the world changed
3. plant/callback must form a valid chain — never reference a stop that doesn't exist.
4. The tour_hook MUST be answerable by closing_revelation.
5. unique_angle must cite a SPECIFIC passage, character, or scene from {{theme_name}}.
6. emotional_beat should mirror the source material's emotional arc where possible.
7. cliffhanger must blend literary curiosity with physical exploration ("around this corner, the scene where…").
8. connecting_thread must braid character motivations (why characters act as they do) with scene context (how this real place shaped or mirrors the fiction). Neither strand alone is sufficient.
9. closing_revelation MUST reference the title of {{theme_name}} and name one specific scene from the work — generic statements about "the power of stories" without a concrete anchor are not acceptable.
10. All locations must be real and factually connected to {{theme_name}}.
