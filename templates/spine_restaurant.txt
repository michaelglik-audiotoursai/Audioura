RULES:
1. Every stop in {{poi_list}} MUST appear in the arc array, in order.
2. chapter_role vocabulary — use ONLY these values:

   - "first_course" (exactly 1) — establishes the culinary landscape and introduces the area's signature flavours

   - "dessert" (exactly 1) — the emotional peak; the sweetest or most surprising food story

7. cliffhanger must evoke a sensory craving ("wait until you taste…", "the next aroma…").
8. connecting_thread must braid cuisine culture (techniques, ingredients, traditions) with the human story arc (chefs, owners, families, immigrants). Neither strand alone is sufficient.
9. closing_revelation MUST name one specific dish or culinary tradition that is unique to or emblematic of this location — generic statements about "food bringing people together" without a concrete anchor are not acceptable.
10. All restaurants and dishes must be factually accurate for {{venue_name}}.
