## What this delivers (D672)

Michael reviewed the emails the app generates when a tour is shared and when a
community invite is sent. Before, a free tour, a paid tour and a community tour
all got one identical message with a long raw tour name ("… with a stop at
Quincy Market and a stop at …") and a long `?n=` link. Now:

- the link is short — `https://audioura.com/t/<CODE>` and nothing else;
- the title is short — "Faneuil Hall Marketplace — Walking Tour", no stop
  clauses, no repeated place;
- the body never lists the stops — it gives the **count** plus the **first
  stop's name and location**;
- free / paid / community each get their own opening line, and the **paid price
  line comes last**;
- the subject is "A \<city\> \<type\> tour for you" (else the generic one);
- the community invite shows the member count only at 2+, and drops the
  "How to get Audioura" / "(links coming soon)" lines.

The exact subjects + bodies, **generated by the real code** from the real DB
stops, are in **`SHARE_EMAIL_SAMPLES_LOCAL-710.md`**.

## 1. One place for the wording — `lib/utils/share_code.dart`

- **`buildShareLink(code)`** → `https://audioura.com/t/<CODE>`. The `?n=<name>`
  query is **gone** everywhere (app + landing page). `buildCommunityInviteLink`
  unchanged (already short).
- **`shortTourTitle(rawName)`** (pure): peels the ` - <Type> Tour` suffix
  (remembering the type), cuts the first `, with a stop at` / ` with a stop at`
  clause and everything after, drops a trailing place tail that repeats/locates
  the place (`, Boston MA` / `, Cleveland, USA` / `, Newton, MA`), then
  re-attaches a normalised ` — <Type> Tour` with the type Capitalised (empty
  type → ` — Tour`). **Never returns empty** — falls back to the trimmed raw
  name. Unicode-safe (Russian names pass through, only the city tail trimmed).
- **`tourTypeFromName`** / **`cityFromLocation`** (pure): derive the subject's
  type (from the ` - <Type> Tour` suffix) and city (from a `…, City, ST ZIP`
  first-stop address, else a `City ST` / `City, ST` tail in the name).
- **`ShareTourDetails`** — the pure input: title, code, kind
  (free/paid/community), price, communityName, stopCount, firstStopName,
  firstStopLocation, city, tourType. A missing stop/location simply omits that
  line; the builder never prints "null".
- **`buildShareMessage(details)`** — exactly the D672 order, one blank line
  between paragraphs, no double spaces, price line **last**:
  1. opening line (by kind); 2. `Hands-free audio: press play and walk.`;
  3. `<N> stops, starting at <first stop> (<location>).`; 4. the short link;
  5. `(Or open Audioura, tap Search and paste <CODE>.)`; 6. community only:
  `Join the <community> community in Audioura to download it.`; 7. paid only,
  last: `The tour price is $<price>.`.
- **`buildShareSubject(details)`** → `A <city> <type> tour for you`
  (type lower-cased) else `An Audioura tour for you`.
- **`buildCommunityInviteMessage(name, code, {memberCount})`** — opening line,
  `<N> members.` only when `memberCount >= 2`, the link, the code fallback.

`extractShareCode` is unchanged and still returns the CODE from every new
message, the bare link, a trailing-slash link, and a legacy `?n=` link (a round
trip is asserted for every sample).
